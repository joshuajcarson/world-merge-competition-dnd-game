# Rarity Budgets (D&D 5.5e / 2024 rules)

Find the tier, build inside it. The **Never exceed** line is a hard stop.

---

## Common

- **Attunement:** almost never.
- **Bonus cap:** none. No +1 to attack, damage, AC, or saves.
- **Spell effects:** cantrip-equivalent, or a 1st-level spell once per day.
- **Charges:** 0–3, "regains all expended charges daily at dawn."
- **Combat impact:** essentially zero. These are utility, convenience, and character.
- **Anchors:** *Potion of Healing* (2d4+2), *Cloak of Billowing*, *Clothes of Mending*.
- **Never exceed:** any persistent numeric bonus, or an effect usable more than once per short rest that saves a resource.

## Uncommon

- **Attunement:** sometimes — roughly a third.
- **Bonus cap:** +1 (weapon, armor, shield, or a single save/skill).
- **Spell effects:** up to 2nd level, limited uses.
- **Charges:** 3–5, "regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn."
- **Combat impact:** noticeable but not defining. The party is glad to have it; nobody reshapes tactics around it.
- **Anchors:** *+1 Weapon*, *Bag of Holding*, *Boots of Elvenkind*, *Wand of Magic Missiles*.
- **Never exceed:** +1 on any single value; two separate +1s stacked onto one item; a 3rd-level spell effect.

## Rare

- **Attunement:** usually.
- **Bonus cap:** +2, or +1 plus a distinct minor property.
- **Spell effects:** up to 3rd level; 4th level only as a once-per-day capstone.
- **Charges:** 4–7, "regains 1d6 + 1 expended charges daily at dawn."
- **Combat impact:** shapes one character's build. Enables a tactic they didn't have.
- **Anchors:** *+2 Weapon*, *Flame Tongue*, *Cloak of Displacement*, *Wand of Fireballs*.
- **Never exceed:** +3 anything; flight; at-will 3rd-level spells; resistance to more than one damage type.

## Very Rare

- **Attunement:** yes, nearly always.
- **Bonus cap:** +3, or +2 plus a significant property.
- **Spell effects:** up to 5th level; 6th only once per day.
- **Charges:** 6–10, "regains 1d6 + 4 expended charges daily at dawn."
- **Combat impact:** defines the character's role. Flight, damage immunity to one type, and ability-score-setting (to 19–21) live here.
- **Anchors:** *+3 Weapon*, *Belt of Fire Giant Strength*, *Staff of Fire*, *Wings of Flying*.
- **Never exceed:** +4 anything; setting an ability score above 21; blanket immunity to a condition with no escape clause; at-will 5th-level spells.

## Legendary

- **Attunement:** yes.
- **Bonus cap:** +3 remains the ceiling. Buy power with *properties*, not with bigger numbers.
- **Spell effects:** up to 7th level; 8th–9th only once per day, with a real cost.
- **Charges:** 10–20, with a recharge risk clause if the item is drained ("if you expend the last charge, roll a d20; on a 1, ...").
- **Combat impact:** an encounter-altering resource. Should come with a story obligation, a sentience, or a drawback.
- **Anchors:** *Holy Avenger*, *Vorpal Sword*, *Staff of the Magi*, *Ring of Three Wishes*.
- **Never exceed:** +4 or higher; at-will 7th-level-or-above spells; unlimited *wish*; permanent immunity to death.

## Artifact

- **Attunement:** yes, and usually conditional on worthiness, oath, or bloodline.
- **Structure:** must have named properties, at least one drawback, and a defined path to destruction.
- **Combat impact:** campaign-level. Not lootable — awarded, discovered as a plot, or inherited.
- **Anchors:** *Book of Vile Darkness*, *Sword of Kas*, *Orb of Dragonkind*.
- **Rule:** never generate one in a batch or hoard. One artifact per campaign arc, and it should carry a story that predates the party.

## 5.5e conventions to match

- Attunement line reads: *"Wondrous Item, Rare (Requires Attunement)"* or *"(Requires Attunement by a Wizard)"*.
- Charge wording: *"This item has N charges and regains 1dX expended charges daily at dawn."*
- Save DCs from items are fixed numbers, not derived from the wielder — state them (typical: DC 13 Uncommon, 15 Rare, 17 Very Rare, 19 Legendary).
- 2024 rules dropped most "on a 1, the item is destroyed" clauses below Legendary — don't add them to lower tiers.
- Weapon masteries belong to the base weapon, not the enchantment. Don't grant a mastery property as the item's magic.
- Prefer "as an action / bonus action / reaction" phrasing and per-day limits over ad-hoc cooldowns.

## Setting overlay

Origin does not change the budget — a leviathan-bone spear and a Faerun longsword at Rare get the same numbers. Origin changes flavor, naming, and drawbacks only. Earth-origin items in particular should be *mundane* mechanically unless the merge has visibly altered them; their strangeness is narrative, not numeric.
