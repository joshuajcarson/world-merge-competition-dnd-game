# Session 7 — The Golden Goose

## Recap

It started with a dream. [Sesug Tsirch](../world/people/sesug-tsirch.md) ("Geezus")
and [Mitch Saddlerash](../world/people/mitch-saddlerash.md) each dreamed of a golden
goose, and a voice told them: *you must find this goose.* They weren't the only ones.
People all over [Briarwood Mall](../world/places/briarwood-mall.md) had the same dream,
so the party set out to answer it.

Before they left, [Jack Mercer](../world/people/jack-mercer.md) pointed out that people
keep making a mess of the mall, and that the Gander had better figure out how to get it
cleaned up soon. Keeping the mall clean had become a focal point for the [Way of the
Gander](../world/factions/way-of-the-gander.md), and the party's downtime this session
went to it.

The party stepped through a portal, meaning only to look at what was going on, and found a group of [Cult of the Swan](../world/factions/cult-of-the-swan.md)
cultists dancing around a fire, along with a band of [Aether Leprechauns](../world/races/aether-leprechauns.md).
The leprechauns carried bags filled with bird parts from the birds they had been
killing, and both groups turned out to love their bird parts. At one point a cultist
summoned the top of a small mountain peak to attack the party for a turn. The
leprechauns were attacking the cultists, but [Phil](../world/people/phil-bernard.md) attacked
the cultists anyway, so the party ended up fighting everyone. By the end only the party and
one cultist were still standing.

They kept that cultist alive and used what he knew to find the golden goose. He ended up living at the mall.

The goose was [Carol](../world/npcs/carol.md), a woman who used to work at a Tiffany's in
New York. She agreed to come back to the mall on three conditions, and the party took the deal:

- access to the mall fountain
- a custom-made goose toilet
- an introduction to [Steve](../world/npcs/steve.md), a Dallas Tiffany's salesman who is
  notorious for cheating on his wife

[The Blood Bowl](../world/lore/the-blood-bowl.md) awaits. In the meantime the party spent
their downtime on long-term projects:

- **Mitch, Jack and Geezus** worked together to get the mall's people to be less dirty, and
  were very successful. Mitch taught sanitation, Jack built a trash compactor, and Geezus
  advocated for vaccines and universal healthcare.
- **[Phil Bernard](../world/people/phil-bernard.md)** worked on his salute. He failed his
  check to learn how to act like a king, again, and made hardly any progress.

## Loose Threads

- The party owes Carol the fountain, a custom goose toilet, and an introduction to Steve.
  She is already at the mall.
- The surviving Cult of the Swan cultist now lives at the mall. His name and what he wants
  aren't recorded.
- What sent the dream and the voice that said to find the goose?
- What role Carol plays in the Way of the Gander now that the golden goose has been found.
- The Blood Bowl is next. Briarwood also carries a demerit from [Session 6](06-the-fixed-game.md).
- Phil's kingship is still not improving.

## DM Only

- **Ran from [The Golden Goose Chase](../encounters/the-golden-goose-chase.md), loosely.** The hook
  changed: the encounter opens on a rumor, and the table got a shared dream instead. The plan
  was to go to the first area and watch the Aether Leprechauns attack the cultists. Phil
  attacked the cultists, so it became a three-way fight, and the surviving cultist led the
  party to Carol.
- **Did not happen:** the quake, Chief [Vainhusk](../world/npcs/vainhusk.md),
  [Gildrot](../world/npcs/gildrot.md), and the Escape node. The named Aether Leprechauns
  ([Gildrot](../world/npcs/gildrot.md), [Prismrot](../world/npcs/prismrot.md)) are unused and
  available later. The quake never started, so the fate of the fragment is open.
- **The Peak.** One of the cultists' attacks was labeled "Peak" instead of "Peck" in the DM's
  notes by accident. The DM kept it and had the cultist summon the top of a small mountain
  peak. It's canon, so Cult of the Swan members can call up peaks.
- **The party attacked rather than parleyed,** though the cult was written as talkable. Phil
  started it. Keeping one alive and using what he knew is what led them to Carol.
- **The Gander is God.** In the Way of the Gander's trinity, "the Gander" is God the Father,
  the goose version. Geezus claims the Gander is his father.
- **Carol's conditions are new canon.** The [Carol](../world/npcs/carol.md) file previously had
  her wanting off the fragment. She now also has a Tiffany's past, a price, and an interest in
  Steve, who was made up by a player on the spot.
- **The voice in the dream** was never identified in the notes. Decide what has that kind of reach.
- **Downtime outcomes** were long-term actions, not scenes. Mitch, Jack and Geezus succeeded at
  the cleanliness campaign; Phil failed his kingship check again.
- **No Session 4 note exists yet,** so the index runs 03, 05, 06, 07.
