# Session 9 — A Glitch in the Signal

## Recap

Leading the surrendered [Hush Concord](../world/factions/the-hush-concord.md) gnomes — "the
ewoks," as the table still calls them — back into [Briarwood Mall](../world/places/briarwood-mall.md),
the party found that the mall basement now had a prison in it. Nobody built it. It was just there,
the way the mall gained a spare janitor's closet the night of the merge, and the gnomes went into it.

With that settled, the party turned north to march on [Meridian Peak](../world/factions/meridian-peak.md)'s
shard — and the hex the Blood Bowl handed them instead looked like a dead ringer for
[Cimarron Flats](../world/places/cimarron-flats.md): the same dust-bowl, Utah-tinged plain, except every
animal on it was Aether-touched. *(See DM Only — this is a different hex wearing the same map.)*

Crossing the open ground, the party split up and the plain tested each of them differently: Mitch
stayed low on stealth, Geezus cleared it on acrobatics, Jack got turned around and confused, and
Phil lost a fight to a patch of cacti. Rhino-sized Aether buffalo grazed the whole way across.

Then a dust cloud rose on the horizon — a stampede, but not a panicked one: a herd moving in a
line, driven by a massive, feather-draped orc carrying a pole wound in swan feathers, aimed
straight at an Agent Pylon. Seeing a Pylon about to fall to what the party clocked immediately as
a stinky swan cultist, they sprinted to beat the herd to it.

At the Pylon they met the orc leading the herd — [Vainhusk](../world/npcs/vainhusk.md) — and a
second cultist, [Anthony](../world/npcs/anthony.md). **IT WAS A DANCE OFF.** Vainhusk and Anthony
clearly thought they had style; they just didn't understand the understated dignity of the Gander.
Mitch channeled his celestial power to show that the true strength of the Gander comes from
within, while Geezus made it extremely clear that he is a notorious **SERG** — a Swan Exclusionary
Radical Ganderist. Vainhusk and Anthony were so impressed that they handed over the Pylon outright
and asked to join the [Church of the Gander](../world/factions/way-of-the-gander.md).

With the Pylon secured, the party found a small shed standing alone in the field. Near it, Jack
found a [Cohort 18 Slag Ring](../items/cohort-18-slag-ring.md). The ground around the shed was
covered in anti-Agent graffiti, including one line that stood out: **"There are more free zones to
the west."** Mitch noticed the air around the shed had a kind of magic static to it — and for the
first time since the merge, nobody could hear Agent at all.

**Everyone leveled up.**

## Loose Threads

- **West.** The shed's graffiti points to "more free zones to the west" — unexplored, and
  explicitly not on the way to Meridian Peak.
- **Agent went silent at the shed.** First time this has happened. Why, and whether Agent noticed
  the gap in its own feed, is unresolved.
- **The mall basement prison.** It wasn't there before and nobody built it. The Hush Concord gnomes
  are in it now.
- **The march on Meridian Peak is still outstanding** — the Pylon hex came first instead.
- **The Cult of the Swan's status as a faction.** Vainhusk and Anthony offered to join the Way of
  the Gander rather than be retired; what that means for the rest of the Cult isn't settled.

## DM Only

- **This hex is not Cimarron Flats.** It was run from [A Glitch](../encounters/a-glitch.md),
  which deliberately reuses the Roll20 Cimarron Flats map for a *different* hex so as not to
  conflict with [Cimarron Flats](../world/places/cimarron-flats.md) itself (Dust Bowl Oklahoma,
  1935, held by the [Black Sunday Kin](../world/factions/black-sunday-kin.md) — a separate,
  already-claimed Holding). The table called the new hex "Cimarron Flats" at the table because it
  recognized the map, not because it's the same place in-fiction. Written up here as its own stub,
  [Featherline Flats](../world/places/featherline-flats.md) — rename it if you'd rather, but don't
  let it quietly merge with the real Cimarron Flats in anyone's notes. A clean in-fiction excuse, if
  you want one: Agent's broadcast reuses set dressing between hexes rather than building each one
  from scratch.
- **Ran as designed.** [A Glitch](../encounters/a-glitch.md)'s Node A (the herd and the Plume),
  Node B (the pageant — resolved as a literal dance-off, Performance/Persuasion win), and Node E
  (the shed, Agent's silence, "go west") all played out close to the file as written. Node C (the
  live Pylon contest) and Node D (the Plume falling/stampede) never triggered — the pageant was won
  clean, so combat never went live. Per the encounter's own **Ending: The Cult Yields**, resolve the
  Cult's refuge request the same way Session 7's lone surviving cultist was handled.
- **Anthony is new.** Not in the prepped file (which only has generic rank-and-file
  [Swan Cultists](../world/npcs/swan-cultist.md)) — the table named one at the Pylon. Stubbed; needs
  a real personality pass if he recurs.
- **The prison appearing in the mall basement** is written here as unexplained on purpose, the same
  register as the extra janitor's closet from the Session 0 merge (see
  [Briarwood Mall](../world/places/briarwood-mall.md) DM Only). Decide later whether it's Agent's
  doing, the Administration's, or something else — don't let it go unaddressed forever.
- **Phase One quota.** Per [The Blood Bowl](../world/lore/the-blood-bowl.md), Phase One needs 2
  Holdings including home. Briarwood now holds its home Pylon plus Featherline Flats — **quota met**
  as of this session, pending whatever Phase Two actually requires.
- **Gaps in the notes:** no detail yet on what the Cult of the Swan's rank and file do now that
  Vainhusk yielded, nor on what Agent says (if anything) about losing the party's feed at the shed.
