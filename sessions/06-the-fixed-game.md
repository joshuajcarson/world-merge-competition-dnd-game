# Session 6 — The Fixed Game

## Recap

The water kept climbing. [Jack Mercer](../world/people/jack-mercer.md)
built a grappling hook on the spot to get the party across to the **Wells
Fargo Regional Office**, where [Silas Renner](../world/npcs/silas-renner.md)
("Silar") and the sniper had fled with the Gamma token.

Inside, the party found the Bahamut pilgrims — the same devotees the table
had been calling "the Bahamut hippies" — still stranded, still without a
token of their own. [Mitch Saddlerash](../world/people/mitch-saddlerash.md)
made them an offer instead of a rescue: that dragons had evolved into the
Gander, and that they belonged at the mall. It worked. The pilgrims agreed
to come to Briarwood, and the party handed them a spare token so they
wouldn't take a demerit for finishing empty-handed. In thanks, the
pilgrims pointed the party upstairs — that's where Silar and his people
had gone — and gave Mitch a can of [Bug Spray](../items/bug-spray.md).

Upstairs, the party found Silar, the sniper, and considerably more company
than expected: kobolds, more of the goop guys, at least one more corrupted
puma, the sniper, Silar himself — eyes hidden behind a pair of glasses
that seemed to do something to anyone who looked too long — and a woman in
a sharply tailored pantsuit who hadn't been part of the fight until now.
The fight went bad fast, in every direction at once. Right after Jack
landed a hit on Silar that nearly ended him outright, the woman in the
pantsuit — **Helena** — called out, and time froze. [Agent](../world/npcs/agent.md)
appeared, declared the Color Game over, and named the Meridian Peak
fragment the winners. Totally fair. Totally not decided in advance.
Helena used the seat's power — the party remembers it as the "Omega"
token, though it's the same Gamma token from the Medical Supplies Factory
— to hand Briarwood a demerit on her way out.

Nobody died. Everyone leveled up for surviving it. In the quiet after,
Geezus started pulling tools from Sears to build an indoor garden;
Mitch tried to help, somehow, with broken glass. Jack started designing a
permanent fix for the mall's skylights. Phil practiced being kingly and
fell on his ass.

## Loose Threads

- **Was the game actually rigged?** Agent ending the fight the instant its
  favorite new villain was about to lose, then declaring the side that
  called for help the winner, reads as favoritism to the party — worth
  deciding deliberately whether that's true, and if so, why Agent would
  bother.
- Silas Renner survived, badly hurt. A grudge, and a rematch, are both on
  the table.
- The sniper is still unidentified to the party.
- The Bahamut pilgrims are now, functionally, new Briarwood residents and
  Way of the Gander converts — how they integrate is open.
- What Briarwood's demerit actually costs, mechanically, isn't decided
  until the Blood Bowl itself plays out.
- Geezus's garden and Jack's skylight project are both in progress, not
  finished.

## DM Only

**Party is now level 4.** Confirmed level-up for surviving the Wells Fargo
fight — rescale any future prep accordingly.

**Reconciliation with prep.** This session is where
[The Long Climb](../encounters/the-long-climb.md) actually resolved, and
it went considerably bigger and messier than designed — see that file's
own "Confirmed in play" note. Short version: the destination building was
Wells Fargo, not Neon Lights Apartment; every Wandering Threat in
[the Color Game](../encounters/the-color-game.md) got thrown into one
combined fight rather than paced separately; and the ending trigger fired
as a public, in-fiction appeal from Marsh to Agent rather than a quiet
"Agent's Snatch" — a more dramatic, more openly biased-looking beat than
what was scripted, and worth keeping as the new canon default.

**Agent's favoritism is now a real, table-raised question**, not just a
DM's private read on the character. See
[Agent](../world/npcs/agent.md)'s file for a new note on this — decide
deliberately whether there's a reason behind it before the party asks
directly.
