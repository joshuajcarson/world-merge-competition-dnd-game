# Session 0 — Welcome to the World Merge Competition

## Recap

Thursday, November 5th, 1992. An ordinary night at
[Briarwood Mall](../world/places/briarwood-mall.md) — the "As Seen On TV"
store's grand opening, [Suzanne Somers](../world/npcs/suzanne-somers.md)
signing autographs and demonstrating the ThighMaster to a line four hundred
deep.

The party were still their pre-merge selves: **Phil Bernard** (Rob), a
fresh-out-of-high-school mall cop living his literal dream job; **Jack
Mercer** (Morgan), the mall's married, middle-aged maintenance man who
tinkers with everything he touches; **Mitch Saddlerash** (Nathan), a mall
performer working the crowd as "Mr. Mime, Jr."; and **Sesug Tsirch** (David),
visiting with a community group, off his medication, and having a rough
night of it.

Trying to calm Sesug down near the signing table, Phil "accidentally"
tackled him off the second-story balcony onto the floor below — and neither
of them was hurt by the fall. Nobody had a good explanation for that.

At the same moment, across the mall, Jack spotted [Dale Pruitt](../world/npcs/dale-pruitt.md) —
fired from mall maintenance days earlier for drinking on shift — bolting out
of Sears with a bag of stolen tools, his girlfriend
[Renee Castillo](../world/npcs/renee-castillo.md) right behind him. Cornered,
Dale and Renee jumped from the second-story balcony too — and fell in slow
motion, drifting down like the building's gravity had opinions about it.

As the chaos peaked — a stranger sailing off a balcony, a mall cop no one
could explain falling unhurt, an autograph line collapsing into a stampede —
a voice nobody recognized cut through everything at once:

> "Welcome to the World Merge Competition."

## Loose Threads

- What actually happened during both falls — Phil and Sesug's, then Dale and
  Renee's. Both landed impossibly soft, both before anyone knew why.
- Dale and Renee are still loose in the mall with a bag of stolen tools when
  the voice speaks.

## DM Only

This is the pre-merge half of what
[Chase Through the Grand Opening](../encounters/chase-through-the-grand-opening.md)
was built to run — that file has more nodes and approaches than got used at
the table; the actual session hit the same core beats (Dale and Renee
spotted, chased, cornered right as the merge triggers) at a faster pace.

The slow-motion falls — both the PC/NPC one (Phil and Sesug) and the
Dale/Renee one — weren't part of the original encounter design. Treat them
as an early, unscripted bleed of the merge's physics, same register as the
extra-closet and extra-door anomalies documented elsewhere (see
[Briarwood Mall](../world/places/briarwood-mall.md)'s DM notes and the
[Deputy Registrar's Log](../world/lore/deputy-registrars-log-scornubel.md)).
Worth deciding whether this kind of thing recurs before the merge properly
lands.

The session ends exactly at the encounter's Node F hard trigger — the World
Merge Event fires as the closing beat and carries straight into
[Session 1](01-mall-rats-and-the-grasslands-city.md).
