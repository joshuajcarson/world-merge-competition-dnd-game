# Session 5 — The Vassal's Bargain

## Recap

Still inside [the Color Game](../encounters/the-color-game.md), Queen
[Miriel Ashgrove](../world/npcs/miriel-ashgrove.md) came to the party
asking for one of their spare tokens, so Scornubel's people could avoid a
demerit of their own. She offered more than thanks for it: a boon from the
Coster Council, brokered through her. [Jack Mercer](../world/people/jack-mercer.md)
wasn't sold — he reminded the party this was the same Council that had
hired the Stewies to raid the mall — and countered with terms of his own.
Jack agreed to hand over the Infrared token (the RC car) on two
conditions: the price for Scornubel's people to use Briarwood's plumbing
goes up from the silver-piece-a-head deal struck back in
[Session 1](01-mall-rats-and-the-grasslands-city.md), and Miriel commits
Scornubel as a vassal state of the mall. Miriel agreed.

With that settled, the party went looking for whoever had shot
[Phil Bernard](../world/people/phil-bernard.md). To cross the water
separating them from the next building, [Geezus](../world/people/sesug-tsirch.md)
glided across and tied off a rope at the **Industrial Chicken Coop**, and
the rest of the party crossed behind him. On the other side, Jack's
spectrometer picked up the Gamma token almost immediately — and he was
rewarded for it with a sniper round to the hip.

Inside the **Medical Supplies Factory**, the party found a knot of droopy,
half-formed "goop guys" and the sniper herself, who broke and ran the
instant she was spotted — the goop guys stayed behind and ruined
[Mitch Saddlerash](../world/people/mitch-saddlerash.md)'s clothes with
their mess on the way. Chasing the sniper deeper into the factory, the
party found the man actually making the goop guys: someone who introduced
himself, or was overheard being called, **Silar**. After a long, running
fight, Silar and the sniper broke for the building's roof — but by the
time the party reached it, both had vanished, and the water had risen
again behind them. Jack's spectrometer picked up one last signal: Silar
and the sniper had relocated, Gamma token in hand, to the **Wells Fargo
Regional Office**.

## Loose Threads

- Whether the Coster Council actually honors Miriel's vassal-state promise
  — or considers her to have overstepped a "Queen" title that was never
  supposed to carry real authority.
- "Silar" is still just a name and a face to the party — no last name, no
  affiliation confirmed.
- The sniper is still completely unidentified.
- Gamma is confirmed at the Wells Fargo Regional Office, guarded by
  whatever's left of Silar's people.

## DM Only

**Name resolution.** "Screnoble" is [Scornubel](../world/places/scornubel.md),
same typo pattern as Session 3. "Silar" is
[Silas Renner](../world/npcs/silas-renner.md), Meridian Peak's Warden —
the "goop guys" are his Duplicate ability, reflavored in play as unstable,
half-formed copies rather than crisp reskinned guards. Treat "Silar" as
the table's own working name for him going forward, the same way "Fearus"
stuck for "Faerun" — correct it only if the table wants to, not by
default.

**The vassal-state deal is a live political problem, not a done deal.**
Miriel has no actual authority to bind Scornubel this way — she's a
Deputy Registrar pressed into a ceremonial crown, not the Coster Council
itself. Whether the Council honors her word, disavows it, or uses it as
leverage against her is a genuinely open, table-shaping thread. See
[Miriel Ashgrove](../world/npcs/miriel-ashgrove.md) and
[The Coster Council](../world/factions/coster-council.md), both updated.

**The plumbing deal, renegotiated.** The original silver-piece-a-head
price (see [Lazare](../world/npcs/lazare.md) and
[Session 1](01-mall-rats-and-the-grasslands-city.md)) just went up, as
Jack's price for the RC car. Exact new figure is the DM's call — pick one
when it matters at the table.

**Reconciliation with prep.** This session runs directly into
[The Long Climb](../encounters/the-long-climb.md)'s Node A/B — see that
file's own "Confirmed in play" note for how the Factory fight and the
escape actually played out against the original design.
