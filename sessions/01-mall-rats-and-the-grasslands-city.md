# Session 1 — Mall Rats and the Grasslands City

## Recap

Time unfroze, and everyone caught in it changed. Some people just — stopped
being there. Phil Bernard became a half-dwarf. Jack Mercer became a Goliath.
Sesug Tsirch became a humanoid goose the table immediately started calling
"Geezus." Mitch Saddlerash became an aasimar. [Dale and Renee](../world/npcs/dale-pruitt.md)
were caught in the same instant, still holding the tools they'd stolen —
Phil ran them down, the party talked them down without a fight, and Phil
confiscated the drill in Renee's hands, which hadn't been an ordinary drill
for a few seconds now.

While Phil calmed the crowd, [Wade Kessler](../world/npcs/wade-kessler.md) —
a DEA agent, by his own account — asked for help clearing the mall as
something large and purple burst through the JCPenney windows: wolf-shaped,
wrong-colored, and already eating someone. See
[Aether Breach at Briarwood](../encounters/aether-breach-at-briarwood.md) —
this is that encounter, reskinned and run within minutes of the merge
rather than weeks later. Phil and Jack fought it off; wounded badly, it
tried to flee and died before it got clear. As it died, time froze again
and a voice — the same one from the balcony — cut in: *"Agent here! Look at
these heroes fight to protect their shitty mall."*

When the voice let go, the mall wasn't in Bellcross anymore. Outside its
windows: an alien forest, trees taller than redwoods, two moons in the sky —
and past that, in another direction entirely, a grassland with a river and a
walled city on the horizon. The building itself had different worlds outside
different windows, and the geometry didn't agree with itself if you walked
too far.

[Phil found a keyring](../items/mall-security-keyring.md) on the ground.
[Jack noticed rooms and doors that hadn't been there before](../world/places/briarwood-mall.md).
Mitch talked the panicking crowd into barricading windows instead of
scattering. And when Jack and Phil opened one of the new doors, time froze a
third time: *"Looks like these heroes have opened the first dungeon — it's
Mall Rats time."*

On the other side: [Sector 7](../world/places/cohort-15-holding-sector.md),
an Administration storage sector for a ratkin population — see
[Mall Rats](../encounters/mall-rats.md). The party fought through the
corridor garrison (Jack rolled a double 1 on advantage and fell into his own
trap), then met [Ironquill](../world/npcs/ironquill.md), a ratkin knight in
full plate holding the checkpoint. Rather than fight to the end, Ironquill
asked for his people's freedom in exchange for his fealty — Mitch closed the
deal, and twenty ratkin walked out of Sector 7 and into Briarwood as its
newest residents. Ironquill explained his people's world was destroyed in
**Cohort 15**, and that the Administration has used them as dungeon fodder
ever since. He led the party onward to Gnashwhisker, who was preparing a
sacrifice — a captive teenager, seventeen-year-old
[Corey Blevins](../world/npcs/corey-blevins.md) — to try to buy the ratkin's
way back into the competition's good graces. The party defeated Gnashwhisker
and freed Corey. As they left, the dungeon door vanished behind them, and
the whole party leveled up.

The rest of that day went to making the mall livable: Jack searched out
secrets, Geezus and Phil worked on security, Mitch started integrating
twenty new ratkin residents into a building that hadn't had any the day
before.

The next day, an elf arrived from the city the party had only ever seen
through the windows — [Lazare](../world/npcs/lazare.md), out of what they'd
been calling the Grasslands City, which turned out to have a real name:
[Scornubel](../world/places/scornubel.md). His own city had been ravaged by
Tiamat's armies. Wade introduced him to Phil. After a long conversation,
Lazare was — genuinely, almost emotionally — impressed by Briarwood's
plumbing: hot water from a tap, a privy that simply flushed itself away. The
party agreed to let Scornubel's people use it for a silver piece a head.

Not long after Lazare left, screaming started at Chess World. The store was
full of naked, gem-studded humanoids attacking anyone without gems of their
own. The party fought through them, and Jack found a portal on the other
side of the fight — a jungle, red gems growing out of everything, and the
voice again: *"Welcome to the Fearus Dungeon."* The party triggered a chain
reaction of exploding gems to blast a clearing through, which drew an
attack from locals furious about their ecosystem getting detonated. Pushing
deeper, they found a duergar encampment forcing captive humanoids to eat the
gems — the source of the attackers back at Chess World — and broke it up.
[The party leveled up again.](../world/places/fearus-dungeon.md)

Some time later, at night, ninjas hit the mall. They were repelled, and
every one of them turned out to be tattooed with the same figure — the
table started calling it "Stevie Griffin" — and nobody yet knew who they
were or who sent them.

## Loose Threads

- Who the tattooed ninjas actually are, and who sent them.
- Whether the Fearus Dungeon's duergar operation is connected to anything
  else the party might run into later.
- The locals who attacked over the destroyed clearing were never identified.
- What Briarwood actually looks like now, geographically, with a forest on
  one side and Scornubel's grassland on another.

## DM Only

**Major deviation from Mall Rats' written endings — now canon.** The
encounter file's endings top out at a handful of ratkin escaping with the
party (**An Uneasy Truce**). What happened at the table went further:
Ironquill's personal fealty plus his entire surviving garrison relocated
into Briarwood as residents. [Ironquill's NPC file](../world/npcs/ironquill.md)
and [Sector 7's place file](../world/places/cohort-15-holding-sector.md)
have both been updated to treat this as settled fact, not an open branch.

**Cohort renumbered 14 → 15** throughout the repo, per Ironquill's own
statement in play. See the renamed
[Sector 7 (Cohort 15 Holding)](../world/places/cohort-15-holding-sector.md).

**Corey Blevins is 17**, not 15 as originally drafted — his file has been
corrected.

**Halric Ammakyl was not used.** [Lazare](../world/npcs/lazare.md) took the
first-contact role that had been drafted for Halric before any session
happened. Halric's file is left alone rather than deleted, but he has not
actually met the party.

**Agent is now a known name to the party**, not just an unattributed voice —
[Agent's NPC file](../world/npcs/agent.md) has been updated to reflect that
the "What the Party Knows" gap has closed.

**The "purple wolf" is [Aether Breach at Briarwood](../encounters/aether-breach-at-briarwood.md),**
folded in rather than treated as a separate incident — reskinned wolf
instead of reptile, JCPenney instead of the fountain court, same session as
the merge instead of weeks later, and the creature died fleeing rather than
fully escaping (closest to that encounter's **Ending: Cornered and
Killed**). See that file's DM notes for the full reconciliation. No
[Cindercrest Commendation Fang](../items/cindercrest-commendation-fang.md)
was claimed at the table.
