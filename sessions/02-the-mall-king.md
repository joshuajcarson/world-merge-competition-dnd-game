# Session 2 — The Mall King

## Recap

The party spent the morning investigating the ninja attack and shoring up
Briarwood's defenses. [Mitch](../world/people/mitch-saddlerash.md) got a
volunteer militia off the ground. [Geezus](../world/people/sesug-tsirch.md)
tried recruiting more of the mall into the
[Way of the Gander](../world/factions/way-of-the-gander.md) — poorly, this
time. [Phil](../world/people/phil-bernard.md) started actually training the
militia. [Jack](../world/people/jack-mercer.md) kept building — splint mail
this round.

Then [Agent](../world/npcs/agent.md) sent word: the **Blood Bowl** is four
days out, and cross-fragment violence is encouraged until then. See
[The Blood Bowl](../world/lore/the-blood-bowl.md) — the clock started this
session.

With rations running low, the party headed out toward Scornubel to learn
more about the ninjas and find food. Outside the mall they found a large
refugee camp already forming; Geezus fed the entire crowd with what looked
like one cinnamon roll and a slice of jerky, and Jack helped put up basic
shelter. The refugees said their towns had been destroyed by Tiamat; Mitch
offered them discounted access to the mall in exchange for pledging support
to the Gander and to Briarwood itself. On the road, the party was ambushed
by giant rats hiding in the grass — fought them off, and found the meat
unexpectedly excellent (eating it functions as a one-time *Hero's Feast*).

Scornubel itself was overcrowded with refugees outside its walls. A bribe
got the party through the gate, where [Lazare](../world/npcs/lazare.md)
found them again. Food was scarce, he said, but the city had a surplus of
weapons and armor — the attack it had spent a season preparing for never
came. Phil bought splint mail and basic defense gear for the militia.
Lazare mentioned that a mercenary company called **the Stewies** had
recently bought up a load of ninja gear, and led the party to their camp in
the mercenary district.

The guard at the Stewies' front gate wasn't impressed by the Gander at
first, but was — after being properly shown its might — thoroughly
converted, and confirmed under intimidation that the company had been hired
by a member of Scornubel's governing Council to hit the mall. The rest of
the company scattered rather than fight. See
[The Stewies](../world/factions/the-stewies.md) and
[The Coster Council](../world/factions/coster-council.md) — the Council has
seven publicly known members and a genuinely opaque decision process, and
which of them hired the attack is still unknown.

Lazare then took the party to a refugee camp at a geyser — matriarchs who'd
lost their gods and had taken up water-worship instead. See
[The Hollow Spring](../world/factions/the-hollow-spring.md). One of the
elders took a real liking to Mitch; Geezus washed an elder's feet as a
courtesy and was given the **Gift of the Hollow Spring** in return — see
[Hallowspring's Thanks](../items/hallowsprings-thanks.md). They had
nothing on the Council, but liked the Gander's energy.

A number of refugees followed the party back to the mall afterward and
joined the growing commune outside it, several converting on the spot.
Between the ratkin, the Gander converts, and the new refugee wave, Briarwood
now has several hundred people camped outside it and roughly 320 inside.
Everyone kept building: Geezus used his earrings to feed the crowd, Mitch
organized squirrel-hunting parties, Jack started on real farm equipment,
Phil finally got the militia functional.

That evening, time froze one more time — Agent, unimpressed, booed the "Mall
People" for falling out of the top 100 channel ratings and threatened an
immediate attack. Monsters came through the recently repaired skylight and
uncomfortably close to a Hot Topic full of orphans. The party and the
militia held, casualties were light, and the fight restocked the mall's meat
supply for days — and drove a spike in militia recruitment.

Later that day, Suzanne Somers — revealed by Agent to be the mall's
preordained "Mall Queen" — asked the party to escort her on a quest to
defeat a duergar queen. She did, with genuine aplomb, and came back charged
with power real enough that the table realized she could credibly challenge
the party's own authority over the mall. Phil resolved this the fastest way
available: he married her, becoming Mall King by marriage. Suzanne, on
realizing she was still canonically married to Alan Hamel back on Earth,
asked for an annulment; Mitch granted one through the Gander in exchange for
her formally abdicating the Mall Queen title — leaving **Phil Bernard as
Briarwood's one and only Mall King**. The whole storyline played very well
with the audience and put the mall's channel back in the top 100.

As the cult around Geezus kept growing, Jack noticed something off about the
arcade — it was leaking water into the mall. Investigating meant going in.
At the center of the arcade dungeon was a giant swan that did not care for
geese. Geezus showed it the Gander's love instead of a fight, and the swan
fled to take its chances in the wild rather than die for it.

## DM Only

**Session numbering note.** The source notes label this "Session 3," with
no "Session 2" ever recorded — likely a mislabel, since the three logged
sessions run on three consecutive dates (Aug 6, 7, 8) with no gap implied.
Filed here as **Session 2** to keep the repo's numbering contiguous with
[Session 0](00-welcome-to-the-world-merge-competition.md) and
[Session 1](01-mall-rats-and-the-grasslands-city.md). Flag if a real,
unlogged session actually happened in between and this should be
renumbered.

**Mall King / Mall Queen is now real governance fact**, not a joke that
stayed contained to the table — Phil Bernard holds it. Worth deciding
deliberately what authority it actually carries in play (see
[Briarwood Mall](../world/places/briarwood-mall.md)'s DM notes, updated with
a pointer here) rather than let it stay purely comedic.

**The Coster Council's own DM notes called this exactly** — the Stewies
contract confirms the Council was always going to be the first organized
Faerun power to treat the mall as a target. Good validation, not a
retcon; build on it.

**Duergar queen, defeated by Suzanne Somers.** Not yet tied to
[the Ashvein Company](../world/factions/ashvein-company.md) or to
[Fearus Dungeon](../world/places/fearus-dungeon.md)'s duergar encampment —
could be either, or a third, separate duergar power. Decide only if the
party asks.
