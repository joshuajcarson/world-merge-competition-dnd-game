# Session 10 — The Wall of Water

## Recap

*Table note: this was Fursonia session. Everyone joined the video chat with a cute animal
background. It had nothing to do with anything in the game.*

Heading west from the shed, the party entered the [Spillway Valley](../world/places/the-spillway-valley.md)
and heard [Agent](../world/npcs/agent.md)'s voice for the first time since the shed. Time froze.
Agent was very upset: from its end the party had simply disappeared, and it tried to force its way
into each character's mind in turn. Everyone came out with a bad headache and a bloody nose. Then
Agent left, and the party carried on.

After a while they saw a massive dam to the north, set into a mountain range. As they moved on, they
reached a series of spillways. [Mitch](../world/people/mitch-saddlerash.md) guessed they were a
graveyard, based on what he knew of how the canals were built in Panama. [Jack](../world/people/jack-mercer.md)
corrected him with an actual understanding of spillways, and noticed that the whole area looked as if
it might be on the verge of collapse.

Crossing a spillway, the party met a man who was a *huge* George H. W. Bush fan, and very upset that
Perot won the election. He seemed confused to learn that Bush's middle name was in fact Herbert
Walker. This was [Roy Teasdale](../world/npcs/roy-teasdale.md). Mitch turned out to be right after
all: spillways are graveyards, and Roy could bring dinosaurs back to life.

[Geezus](../world/people/sesug-tsirch.md) immediately showed Roy the holy power of the Geezus, and
Roy called out to Agent for help. The fight carried on, and an explosion went off in the distance, at
the dam. As the party got ready to take Roy out, a wall of water arrived. It was a massive blue wave.

## Loose Threads

- **The wave.** The session ended as it hit. What happens to the party is unresolved.
- **The dam.** It exploded in the middle of the fight. Who or what set it off, and what was happening
  up there, is unknown.
- **Roy.** He called Agent for help and was still standing when the water arrived. The fight with
  him is unfinished.
- **Agent's mind intrusion.** Agent tried to get inside each character's head and left everyone with
  a nosebleed. Why it lost track of the party in the first place is still open.
- **The valley on the edge of collapse.** Jack saw it before the dam went.
