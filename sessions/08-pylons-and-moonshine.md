# Session 8 — Pylons and Moonshine

## Recap

The session opened with blood running down the walls of [Briarwood Mall](../world/places/briarwood-mall.md).
[Agent](../world/npcs/agent.md) played an ad in which the creator of the competition
revealed that the World Merge Competition matters because it produces the aether that keeps
the creator's society running.

Agent then explained the next rule of [The Blood Bowl](../world/lore/the-blood-bowl.md): every
shard will have a [Pylon](../world/lore/pylons.md) in its base. Each shard must protect its own
and steal Pylons from other shards.

The mall's congregation started to panic. [Gary](../world/npcs/gary.md) came running from the
[Mattress King](../world/places/mattress-king.md), the mall store where the party sleeps, and said that wolves had *(rest of his report
not recorded)*. He also admitted that he and [Dale](../world/npcs/dale-pruitt.md) had been
making moonshine in the Sears, and worried that Dale might get killed by the wolves.

Worried the wolves might wreck the moonshine once they were done with Dale, [Mitch](../world/people/mitch-saddlerash.md)
leaped into action. The Sears was full of [Aether Wolves](../world/races/aether-wolves.md).
Mitch used the skills he picked up at the range and made a nice wolf stew out of
[the Alpha](../world/npcs/alpha-aether-wolf.md).

More importantly, **the moonshine was saved**, and most of Dale's setup was left intact. The party
confiscated Dale's moonshine for lack of permitting. It was Mitch who mostly wanted all of it for
himself, but he got the party to do the confiscating. [Phil](../world/people/phil-bernard.md), as Mall
King, would issue Dale a permit later so he could keep making it. [Jack](../world/people/jack-mercer.md) also found
a single-use [Polaroid One Step](../items/polaroid-one-step-last-exposure.md) as a reward.

On the way back, an earthquake hit, and Mitch spilled all of the moonshine, thanks to his shitty
old legs.

Agent then appeared again and announced that winners of old competitions are joining the game,
including the undefeated [Bar Bar clan](../world/factions/bar-bar-clan.md). Right after, a
60-foot-tall giant sloth and a group of ewoks appeared in the parking lot and started messing with
the mall's Pylon. The ewoks, who the table thought looked more like ewoks than gnomes, were the
[Hush Concord](../world/factions/the-hush-concord.md) gnomes the party had fought before. They really
did not like Phil. The sloth, which is [Deacon](../world/npcs/deacon.md), seemed uninterested in the
party, other than eating all of the corpses.

After the party reminded them that messing with Briarwood's Pylon is very rude, the ewoks
surrendered. The sloth went on snacking on the ewoks who didn't surrender in time. **The Pylon
was saved.**
