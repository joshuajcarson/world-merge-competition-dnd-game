# Dale Pruitt

## Description

Twenty-six, wiry, in Briarwood Mall maintenance until earlier this week, when one
of the party (as their pre-merge Session 0 selves — the DM should decide or ask
which PC's job this was) fired him for drinking on shift. He's spent the days since
getting angrier and drunker in the parking lot, and tonight he's back — not for
revenge exactly, more because [Renee Castillo](renee-castillo.md) talked him into
one bad idea: walk into the open Sears auto center while everyone's distracted by
the ThighMaster crowd and walk back out with a bag of tools. Sears was never his
employer — mall maintenance was, and getting fired from it is what put him in the
mood to say yes to Renee's idea in the first place.

## What the Party Knows

He's running out of the [Sears auto center](../places/briarwood-mall.md) with a
stolen tool bag, Renee at his side, and a mechanic behind him yelling "thief." See
[Chase Through the Grand Opening](../../encounters/chase-through-the-grand-opening.md)
for how the party first meets him.

## DM Only

He's not dangerous — he's humiliated and a little drunk, and getting caught matters
more to his pride than to any actual stakes. That reads through even after the merge:
see the encounter file's Node G for how his transformed stance (defensive, shielding
Renee, not advancing) should telegraph that he'd rather this end without a fight.

Post-merge he becomes what the encounter calls a **"Wizard's Guard"** — the
transformation reads his stolen tire iron (or torque wrench, DM's pick from the tool
bag) as a weapon and wards him as Renee's protector, since that's the role he's
physically in when the merge hits. Mechanically: reskin the Monster Manual **Guard**
statblock (CR 1/8), sturdier than Renee, melee-focused, with one usable
ward/shield-adjacent feature tied to standing near her. See the encounter's Combat
Notes for sizing against a level 1 party.

**Resolved in [Session 1](../../sessions/01-mall-rats-and-the-grasslands-city.md):**
the party talked Dale and Renee down without a fight. Phil Bernard confiscated
Renee's transformed drill (see her file) and let them both go rather than
detain them. Both are alive, unconfined, and a ready-made hook for later —
whether they've stayed near Briarwood or scattered isn't established yet.
