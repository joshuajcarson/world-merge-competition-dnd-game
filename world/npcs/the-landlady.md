# The Landlady

## Description

Larger and slower than a worker Warrenwright, built for endurance and
command rather than digging — she rarely leaves the deepest chamber of her
warren. "The Landlady" is Cohort 23's broadcast nickname for her, not
anything she or her colony would recognize; Agent hands out names like this
to anything it thinks makes good recurring television. See
[Warrenwrights](../races/warrenwrights.md) for the race this individual
belongs to.

## What the Party Knows

Nothing. Not yet encountered.

## DM Only

**Function.** A Broodqueen doesn't fight and shouldn't be built to. Her
value at the table is that she can order a Release from any Warrenwright in
her colony on her turn, without that Warrenwright needing to be bloodied
first — the same escalation lever [Priya Anand](priya-anand.md) gives the
Fulgurites. Use her to turn a straightforward warren fight into a fight the
party has to end fast, by cutting off her line of command to the rest of the
colony.

**Needs before use.** Statblock (leader/support tier, well above the CR
1/2 worker band — bugbear captain or hobgoblin warlord tier is a reasonable
start), and a specific warren to seat her in. Left unset deliberately until
a real encounter calls for a Warrenwright colony.
