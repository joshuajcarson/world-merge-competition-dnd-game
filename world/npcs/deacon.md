# Deacon

## Description

The biggest Sextonback anyone has seen, with a bone-chime ridge so long it
sounds like a church bell being dragged down a gravel road. One of its chimes is cracked
and rings flat. Everyone who hears it comes to recognise that note.

## What the Party Knows

Nothing yet. Expect them to hear it before they see it.

## DM Only

Use the adult block in [Sextonbacks](../races/sextonbacks.md). Deacon wanders the
Blood Bowl's battlesites on a loop Agent steers with scent and broadcast
lighting; it turns up at the mall parking lot, the Cimarron Flats and anywhere
else a fight produces bodies. It has never attacked anyone and does not intend
to start. **Agent will protect Deacon**: harm to it brings an on-air scolding and
a ratings penalty, and a debt the party will feel in a later phase.

Deacon's cracked chime is a free tell. When you want the table to realise that
bodies are about to start vanishing, ring it.
