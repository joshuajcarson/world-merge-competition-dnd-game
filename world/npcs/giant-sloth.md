# The Giant Sloth

## Description

Sixty feet tall. Showed up in Briarwood's parking lot in
[Session 8](../../sessions/08-pylons-and-moonshine.md) with a group of ewoks. Disinterested in the
party, but it ate every corpse in reach, including the ewoks who didn't surrender in time.

## What the Party Knows

It eats the dead and ignores the living, at least this time.
