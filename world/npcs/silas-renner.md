# Silas Renner

## Description

Built like the compound's own gym equipment, tactical vest worn like a
second skin, never raises his voice because he has never once needed to.
Where [Helena Marsh](helena-marsh.md) frames Meridian Peak's cruelty as
policy, Renner is the one who actually enforced it, pre-merge — the man
who decided, quietly and without ceremony, which residents of the compound
counted as an asset and which didn't. He carries two pieces of
compound-issue field tech, warped by the merge the same way Wade Kessler's
DEA badge was: a neural compliance rig, originally built to keep the
compound's own residents in line, and a field-medical replication unit,
originally built to print emergency tissue grafts. Neither does what it
was designed to do anymore.

## DM Only

**What he actually is.** Not a fanatic, not a sadist — a systems person.
Renner doesn't hate anyone he's ever hurt; he just doesn't weigh their pain
against Meridian Peak's continuity, the same bloodless math Marsh runs
with better PR. He is, in his own accounting, the reason the compound has
survived every crisis before this one, and he intends to be the reason it
survives this one too.

**His job tonight, specifically.** Buy Marsh time to get clear with
whatever Meridian Peak is holding, Gamma included if they have it. He will
spend guards, duplicates, and if it comes to it his own life on that job
without hesitating — not out of loyalty exactly, more the same
systems-logic that's run his whole life: Marsh escaping is the
highest-value outcome available, so it's what he optimizes for.

**Compliance Rig (Dominate).** A merge-warped neural-compliance device —
see [The Long Climb](../../encounters/the-long-climb.md)'s Combat Notes for
the full mechanic. Designed to keep compound residents docile during
triage; now functions as a real, if temporary and breakable, charm effect
that can be turned on a party member mid-fight. Play it as clinical, not
menacing — Renner doesn't gloat when it lands, he just redirects whoever's
charmed like he's reassigning a task.

**Replication Unit (Duplicate).** The other half of his kit — see the same
encounter file. Spits out short-lived, weaker copies of whatever Meridian
Peak combatant he touches, which is most of how "the Queen brought far
more people than anyone else" actually works mechanically. The duplicates
are visibly wrong up close — flat affect, a faint grey cast to the skin —
but read as real enough at a glance and in bad light to do their job.

**Statblock notes.** Full stat block in
[The Long Climb](../../encounters/the-long-climb.md)'s Combat Notes — CR 4,
built as this fight's actual combat threat since Marsh herself never
engages. Reskin flavor: security-issue tactical gear, a shock baton, no
firearm (Meridian Peak's rules, not a limitation of the merge).

**Playing him long-term.** Survives this encounter by design — see that
file's escalation trigger. A genuine returning threat if the campaign wants
one: competent, patient, and not remotely finished being useful to Marsh.
Unlike her, he has no interest in being liked, which makes him harder to
negotiate with and easier to respect.
