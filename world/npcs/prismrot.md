# Prismrot

## Description

Looks like any other Leprechaun at a glance — small, wiry, green, too many
teeth — until it turns to look at something, and its eyes catch the light
wrong: a swirling, oily rainbow instead of any color an eye should be. The
rest of a Luck gives it space without being told to. Nobody's ever
explained why; they just do.

## What the Party Knows

Nothing yet. Not currently tied to any confirmed encounter — see DM Only
for how to bring it in.

## DM Only

**What the rainbow eyes actually are.** Unexplained on purpose, same as
everything else about why some Leprechauns carry Doombows and most don't
(see [Aether Leprechauns](../races/aether-leprechauns.md)) — this is
Gold Hunger's opposite number, gone strange: instead of tracking gold, a
prism-touched Leprechaun seems to have tuned into something under Aether's
raw ambient energy that reads as color instead of luck. Don't explain it
further at the table; a Luck reacting to it with instinctive deference is
enough.

**Statblock.** Reskin a **Goblin Boss** (CR 1) with
[Aether Leprechauns](../races/aether-leprechauns.md)' Gold Hunger and
Ravenous Bite traits layered on, plus, in place of a Doombow:

**Chromatic Burst.** *Ranged Spell Attack:* +4 to hit, range 60 ft., one
target. *Hit:* 2d6 damage — roll a d6 to determine the type: 1 acid, 2
cold, 3 fire, 4 lightning, 5 poison, 6 thunder. Prismrot doesn't choose the
type and can't control it; neither does the DM. Roll fresh every time it
hits.

**Play the randomness up.** This isn't a tactical ability — it's Gold
Hunger's luck reflex turned into elemental chaos instead of treasure-sense.
Prismrot doesn't strategize around what type comes up; it just keeps
shooting and trusts its own luck the way Gildrot trusts its nose for gold.

**Where to use this.** Not written as part of
[the Preening Grounds](../places/the-preening-grounds.md) raid —
[Gildrot](gildrot.md) already leads that Luck. Use Prismrot for a
different Luck entirely, or introduce it as a mid-mission escalation
(reinforcements arriving, a rival claimant to Gildrot's leadership) if a
table wants a twist later.

**Token Prompt.** Campaign default art style is Glitch-Corrupted Render —
see `.claude/skills/campaign-chronicle/references/monster-art-style.md`.
Same body as a rank-and-file Leprechaun; only the eyes (and the
corruption's own color) change.
