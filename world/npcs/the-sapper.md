# The Sapper

## Description

A worker-tier Warrenwright found well outside home territory, holding a
stretch of Faerun riverbank the way its colony would hold ground on Aether —
dammed, undercut, and quietly rewired to its own advantage. "The Sapper" is
Cohort 23's broadcast nickname, not a name it or any Warrenwright would use
for itself. See [Warrenwrights](../races/warrenwrights.md) for the race, and
its Environmental Adaptations section for the Faerun River traits (Amphibious,
Borrowed Current, Release — Floodbreak) this individual runs on.

## What the Party Knows

Nothing. Not yet encountered.

## DM Only

**Function.** The field example for a Warrenwright met alone, away from a
full colony — good for a smaller river encounter (a dam that's mysteriously
appeared, livestock going missing at a ford) before the party has any reason
to know the race exists. Escalate to a full colony, with
[The Landlady](the-landlady.md) seated somewhere upstream, if the hook lands.

**Needs before use.** A specific river and settlement to attach to — nothing
in the current campaign geography is confirmed to have one yet. Scornubel or
a stretch of the Chionthar are plausible options, not commitments.
