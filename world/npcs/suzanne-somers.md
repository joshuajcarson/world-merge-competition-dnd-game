# Suzanne Somers

## Description

Television actress, appearing in person at [Briarwood Mall](../places/briarwood-mall.md) on the evening of November 5th, 1992 to demonstrate the ThighMaster at the "As Seen On TV" grand opening. Professionally warm, tireless with a line of four hundred people, and working a room she is far too famous for.

## What the Party Knows

Survived the merge. In [Session 2](../../sessions/02-the-mall-king.md), Agent
revealed her to be Briarwood's preordained "Mall Queen" and sent her on a
quest with the party to defeat a duergar queen — which she did with real
skill, and came back from visibly charged with power, enough that the table
read her as a genuine challenge to the party's own authority over the mall.
Phil Bernard resolved it by marrying her, becoming Mall King. She then
recalled she was still canonically married to Alan Hamel back on Earth and
asked for an annulment; Mitch granted one through the
[Way of the Gander](../factions/way-of-the-gander.md) in exchange for her
formally abdicating the Mall Queen title. She's still at the mall, no longer
holding any formal title, having generated what the broadcast treated as
some of the best ratings of the week doing it.

## DM Only

**No longer a stub — she's now load-bearing.** The "Mall Queen" title and
the power charge she came back with from the duergar fight were never
explained mechanically or narratively beyond "Agent said so" — worth
deciding deliberately what that power actually was/is, and whether
abdicating the title also cost her whatever she gained, or whether she's
still quietly more dangerous than she looks.

**Alan Hamel is a real fact about the real Suzanne Somers**, used here as an
in-fiction joke that landed well at the table — her canonical Earth marriage
is the reason the "Mall King" marriage got annulled rather than stuck. Keep
playing that beat straight; it's funnier for being true.

**Tone going forward:** still the most recognizable Earth face in the
building, still a broadcast favorite — but no longer a background stub.
She has opinions now about Phil (the annulment was amicable at the table,
but "briefly married the mall cop, then had it annulled by a goose god" is
real history between them worth letting color future scenes) and about her
own relationship to whatever power the duergar fight left her with.
