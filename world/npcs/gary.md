# Gary

## Description

Forty-something, out of shape, built like a man who has lifted transmissions his whole
life and has the back to prove it. He ran across Briarwood on the night of the merge
yelling "Thief! They stole my tools!" Keeps his tools in a numbered rack and always
knows which one is missing.

## What the Party Knows

He is the Sears mechanic who chased [Dale](dale-pruitt.md) and
[Renee](renee-castillo.md) in
[Chase Through the Grand Opening](../../encounters/chase-through-the-grand-opening.md).

## DM Only

**Stub.** Added 2026-10-08. Dale returned the tools one at a time and Gary gave him a
cot in the back of the bay. They are friends now, which is the single most
embarrassing thing either of them has ever done. Gary is the only mall resident who
knows Dale and Renee were the thieves, and the only person Dale would ask for help.
No statblock needed: a **Commoner** with strong opinions about a clean tool rack.
