# Vainhusk

## Description

Draped in more feathers than any single bird could plausibly have
supplied, worn with the total confidence of someone who has never once
been told they look ridiculous — and among the Cult, nobody would dare.
Genuinely, sincerely convinced that beauty is worth dying for, and worth
killing for first.

## What the Party Knows

Nothing yet — first contact is
[The Golden Goose Chase](../../encounters/the-golden-goose-chase.md).

## DM Only

**Statblock.** Reskin an **Orc War Chief** or **Half-Ogre**-tier base
(CR 2-3) with no unusual traits added — Vainhusk is dangerous because
they're a big, motivated orc with a lot invested in this fight, not
because of any special ability. Keep it that simple; the faction's flavor
does the work, not the stat block.

**What they'd do under pressure.** Negotiate if the offer is genuinely
about beauty or prestige — a trade, a trophy, an alliance against the
Leprechauns ruining the tribe's hunting grounds. Fight if insulted,
mocked, or denied outright. Vainhusk has never had to back down and
doesn't know how to do it gracefully; a party looking for a clean
diplomatic win should give them a face-saving way to stand down rather
than asking for unconditional surrender.

**The golden goose, specifically.** Vainhusk has heard it described as the
most beautiful bird anyone's ever seen and wants it more than anything
else on the Preening Grounds right now — has no idea it's magical, sapient,
or a transformed person, and would be genuinely disturbed to learn that
mid-hunt. A good pressure point if the party wants to talk them out of the
chase rather than fight over it.
