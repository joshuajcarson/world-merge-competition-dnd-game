# Verger

## Description

A Sextonback calf the size of a minivan: all legs, too-big feet, and a chime
ridge that has only a few small, tinny bells. It follows [Deacon](deacon.md),
and when it loses Deacon it panics in a way a creature this size should not be
allowed to.

## What the Party Knows

Nothing yet.

## DM Only

**Statblock.** Use the adult block in [Sextonbacks](../races/sextonbacks.md),
scaled down: Large beast, about CR 1 (200 XP), **AC** 13, **HP** 45 (6d10+12),
**Speed** 30 ft. Slam +5, 1d12+3 bludgeoning. If hit, it Dashes away in a panic rather
than fighting a second round. It eats
corpses one size category faster than an adult can, not slower: a calf is
*more* enthusiastic.

**Use.** Verger is the complication that wanders in from the wrong side: it
rushes toward a body in the middle of a fight, or hides under a vehicle, or
follows a party member who fed it. It is not a threat unless somebody hits it,
and then it brings Deacon.
