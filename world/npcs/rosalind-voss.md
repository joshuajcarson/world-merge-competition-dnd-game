# Rosalind Voss

## Description

Late thirties, Bellcross Parks and Recreation, the one who filled the feed
bins every morning before the lake opened to the public. She was ankle-deep
at the water's edge with a bucket when the merge landed — close enough that
whatever the fusion did to the flock did to her too. What's left is still
recognizably a person from the neck up, in the way the rest of the Pen isn't:
she still favors her old feed bucket over anything else she's found, still
orients toward the boathouse the way she would toward a break room, and
she's the only one of the flock who has, so far, backed off from a fight
instead of finishing it.

## What the Party Knows

Nothing yet — the flock hasn't been encountered.

## DM Only

**Function.** The first-contact figure for
[the Drowned Chantry](../places/drowned-chantry.md), doing for that dungeon
what [Halric Ammakyl](halric-ammakyl.md) does for
[Scornubel](../places/scornubel.md): the individual who makes the first
meeting a conversation instead of a wipe, if the party gives her the chance.
She can't speak, but she can be read — Insight, not Persuasion, is the
relevant check for whether the party notices she's stalling rather than
attacking.

**What she wants.** Nothing coherent enough to negotiate over. She wants
people to leave the water alone, the way she wanted rowdy teenagers to leave
the water alone when this was still a job. Give her a moment where the party
can either back off and let her de-escalate the rest of the Pen, or push
past her and force the fight — that choice is the whole point of her
existing as a named individual instead of one more Swanfolk.

**Open question:** whether she's ever reachable by the "cure" hook mentioned
in [Swanfolk](../races/swanfolk.md)'s DM notes, or whether that's reserved
for [Sister Maerwyn](sister-maerwyn.md) alone, is undecided — pick whichever
serves the table if the party goes looking.
