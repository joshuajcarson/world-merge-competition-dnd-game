# Carol

## Description

An ordinary goose to look at, if a goose could somehow look exhausted and
extremely done with everyone's shit — and then she opens her mouth, which
geese should not be able to do, and it turns out she has a lot to say
about it. Lays an egg roughly the size and weight of a real one, solid
gold, on a schedule she doesn't control and has stopped being delighted
by. She has been running from something with feathers on it for what
feels like her whole new life.

## What the Party Knows

Nothing yet — first contact is
[The Golden Goose Chase](../../encounters/the-golden-goose-chase.md).

## DM Only

**What actually happened to her.** Same merge moment as every other Earth
transformation in this campaign — she just didn't get a race, she got
routed through something closer to a class, and the wrong one. She meant
to pick "Gilder" — a gold-and-wealth-themed caster, exactly the kind of
flashy, powerful-sounding option she wanted — and the interface, sorted
alphabetically, put "Glider" right next to it. She fat-fingered the
selection under pressure, at the worst possible second, and got both
effects folded into one outcome instead of the one she meant: Glider's
flight became literal, avian flight; Gilder's wealth-magic became literal,
golden eggs. Nobody designed this. It's a merge accident wearing the shape
of a fairy tale, and she knows exactly how it happened and finds it
completely unfunny.

**Playing her.** Sharp-tongued, exhausted, and correctly furious — this
should read as a real person's bad week, not a whimsical fairy-tale
creature. She's spent her transformation being hunted for parts by people
who can't even correctly identify what species she is, which she finds
almost more insulting than the transformation itself. Quick to sarcasm,
quicker to trust a party that treats her like a person and not a prize.

**What she wants.** Off this fragment before the quake finishes it,
obviously — but also, eventually, someone to explain what "Gilder" and
"Glider" would each have actually done, because right now she just has
the worst parts of both and none of the class fantasy either one
promised. This is a legitimate long-term hook if the table wants to
develop her: is there a way to actually finish becoming a Gilder, shed the
Glider half, or is the fusion permanent?

**Statblock.** No PC levels; not built as a combatant. If forced to defend
herself, she can fly (per the Glider half of the accident) and her only
offensive option is an ordinary goose bite — treat her as harmless in a
fight and dangerous only to morale if someone insults her further. She is
meant to be an escort objective, not an encounter.

**The eggs.** One appears roughly once per day, real, solid gold, worth a
genuine sum — not an infinite resource (she isn't a goldmine, she's a
person this happened to), but a fair, occasional reward if the party keeps
her safe and treats her decently rather than trying to farm her.
