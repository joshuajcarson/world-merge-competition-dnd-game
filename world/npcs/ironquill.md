# Ironquill

## Description

A ratkin in full plate, first met guarding the checkpoint deep in
[Sector 7](../places/cohort-15-holding-sector.md) — see
[Mall Rats](../../encounters/mall-rats.md) for the dungeon he used to guard.
Proud, plainspoken, and done being anyone's disposable content.

## What the Party Knows

He surrendered rather than fought to the last, and asked for something no
approach in the DM's original prep anticipated: safe passage out of the
sector for his people, in exchange for his fealty. The party accepted. Twenty
ratkin — the survivors of Sector 7's garrison — now live inside Briarwood
Mall as part of its community, and Ironquill answers to the party directly.

## DM Only

**Not in the original design.** [Mall Rats](../../encounters/mall-rats.md)'s
written endings top out at a handful of ratkin coming out through the seam
(**Ending: An Uneasy Truce**). What actually happened at the table went
further: full fealty from Ironquill personally, and his entire remaining
garrison relocated into the mall as residents. Treat this as the campaign's
actual canon going forward — Sector 7's ratkin are now Briarwood's ratkin.
See [Session 1](../../sessions/01-mall-rats-and-the-grasslands-city.md) for
the scene.

**Cohort correction.** Ironquill states his people's homeworld was destroyed
in **Cohort 15**, not Cohort 14 as originally drafted — the sector and its
tags have been renamed to match (see
[Sector 7 (Cohort 15 Holding)](../places/cohort-15-holding-sector.md)).

**Playing him now.** He's a sworn vassal, not a mascot — pride intact, aware
his fealty was a negotiated exchange rather than a rescue, and the ranking
authority among the mall's ratkin population. A strong candidate to lead the
militia's ratkin contingent once Briarwood starts organizing real defense
(see Briarwood Mall's DM notes on the militia). What he wants out of this
arrangement long-term — citizenship, revenge on the Administration, just
somewhere safe — is worth developing in play rather than deciding here.
