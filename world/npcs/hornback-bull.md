# The Hornback Bull

## Description

Half again the size of the cows, with a heavy brow of scarred white horn and cracked, glittering armor plates. The violet
glow on its plates burns brighter than the rest of the herd. It is the one animal in the herd that does not entirely
need the Plume, and the Cult knows it.

## What the Party Knows

Nothing yet. First contact is [A Glitch](../../encounters/a-glitch.md).

## DM Only

Part of the [Aether Hornbacks](../races/aether-hornbacks.md). Arrives in round 4 of the Pylon Contest if the
Plume is still standing.

### Hornback Bull (CR 2, 450 XP)

*Large beast, unaligned.* **AC** 15 (natural armor) · **HP** 68 (8d10+24) · **Speed** 40 ft.
STR 20 (+5) DEX 10 (+0) CON 17 (+3) INT 2 (-4) WIS 12 (+1) CHA 6 (-2).
**Senses** passive Perception 11. **Languages** none.
- **Aether Shimmer.** Dim violet light out to 15 feet.
- **Charge.** If it moves at least 20 feet straight at a target and hits with Gore, +2d6 piercing damage and a DC 14
  Strength save or knocked prone.
- **Aether Flare (1/Day, when first bloodied).** Each creature within 10 feet makes a DC 13 Constitution save, taking 2d6
  force damage on a failure, half on a success. The Bull is not Lured while flared.
- **Gore.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 15 (2d8+6) piercing damage.

It breaks off if the Plume falls. It does not break off for anything else.
