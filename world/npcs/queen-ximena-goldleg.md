# Queen Ximena Goldleg

## Description

A palm-sized golden frog on a throne carved from a single lily pad, carried
by four attendants, with a crown of dewdrops that never dries. She speaks in a
huge, sonorous voice that belongs to something fifty times her size, and it
carries.

## What the Party Knows

Nothing yet.

## DM Only

Treat her as a social encounter first. If it turns to blows, stat her as a
**Swarm of Poisonous Snakes**-scale hazard (a fast, poisonous skirmisher, about
CR 2) with Contact Poison: DC 13 Con save or poisoned for 1 minute. Her Court
adds blowgun darts (+4, 1d4 piercing, poison DC 12). She grades every
performance out of ten, out loud, and the score is binding.
