# The Pit Boss

## Description

A Warrenwright scout that ended up somewhere with no magic at all and
adapted anyway — living inside the walls and ceiling voids of an Earth
casino, chewing through wiring the way its colony would chew through roots
at home. "The Pit Boss" is Cohort 23's broadcast nickname, the kind of joke
Agent can't resist handing a creature that's quietly taken over a casino
floor's electrical guts. See [Warrenwrights](../races/warrenwrights.md) for
the race, and its Environmental Adaptations section for the Earth Casino
traits (Live-Wire Claws, Blackout Reflex, Release — Short Circuit) this
individual runs on.

## What the Party Knows

Nothing. Not yet encountered.

## DM Only

**Function.** The field example for a Warrenwright met somewhere the party
has zero reason to expect one — a magic-less Earth casino is as far from
"Aether monster" as this campaign's geography gets. Good for a location
where things have been going wrong in small, electrical ways (flickering
lights, machines paying out or failing at odd times, breakers tripping)
before anyone realizes it's a creature and not faulty wiring.

**Needs before use.** A specific casino to attach to — nothing in current
campaign geography is confirmed to have one yet; Bellcross's timeframe
(November 1992, small-town Texas) makes a full casino a stretch without
justification, so this is written as a portable "wherever the party finds a
casino later" hook rather than tied to Briarwood.
