# Helena Marsh

## Description

Late fifties, dressed like she's still running a board meeting — pressed,
composed, unhurried in a way that reads as more unsettling than panic would.
She has been waiting her whole life for a crisis to hand her permission she
already believed she had. [Agent](agent.md)'s royalty decree didn't change
her mind about anything; it just gave her a title to say out loud.

## What the Party Knows

Nothing yet — first contact is [the Color Game](../../encounters/the-color-game.md).

## DM Only

**Not a screamer.** She never raises her voice, never personally threatens
anyone. Her security detail does the physical work; she does the framing —
"viable," "sustainable," "difficult but necessary" — and genuinely believes
every word of it.

**What she'd do under pressure.** Retreat, not fight, the instant the optics
turn against her — she is still performing for the same broadcast Agent is
running, and a Sovereign who dies swinging on camera is worse television, by
her own accounting, than one who withdraws and comes back better prepared.
Order her guards to fall back before she'd order them to die for one token.

**Statblock notes.** No PC levels; a command/social presence, not a
combatant. Reskin a **Noble** or **Spy** (CR 1) with a persuasive or
command-flavored feature layered on — she directs the fight, she doesn't
join it. See [the Color Game](../../encounters/the-color-game.md)'s Combat
Notes for her security detail.

**Playing her long-term.** If Meridian Peak wins their Color Game seat, she
is a real, returning antagonist with the standing to make her Hazard
Nomination stick. If the party takes the seat and the manifest from her
instead, she loses the title before she's had it a full day — worth playing
that loss as humiliating and motivating, not fatal; she survives to resent
it.
