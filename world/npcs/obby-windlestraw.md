# Prelate Obby Windlestraw

## Description

A very old gnome, small even by gnome standards, who hovers a hand's breadth off
the ground on a cushion of air that makes his pale robes ripple when nothing else
for a mile is moving. He carries a lace handkerchief that he flicks at the air before
he speaks, a habit from a world where speech spends breath and he has never
quite stopped counting. His voice is soft, courtly and a little out of puff, and
every sentence ends as if he were asking permission.

## What the Party Knows

Nothing yet. First contact is
[The Demerit Comes Due](../../encounters/the-demerit-comes-due.md).

## DM Only

**Motive.** He wants to be a good winner. He took the Prince's bargain at nineteen,
has paid his tithe every day for sixty years, and believes without irony that
the Calm saved his people from something worse. He would rather walk the party
into the fountain court as guests than as corpses. He will also, politely and
without hesitation, burn the mall's doors off their frames if they refuse.

**Under pressure.** He gets *quieter*. A shouting Obby is a bluff. A hushed Obby
with a drawn breath is about to use Stolen Breath on the person he likes least.
He will not die for the Pylon: down to a quarter of his hit points he asks, very
courteously, for a "mutually agreeable intermission".

**Statblock.** Small humanoid, lawful evil. About CR 4 (1,100 XP).

- **AC** 15 (Wind Veil) · **HP** 52 (8d6+24) · **Speed** 25 ft., fly 30 ft. (hover)
- STR 8 DEX 14 CON 16 INT 14 WIS 16 CHA 13
- **Saves** Con +6, Wis +6 · **Skills** Religion +4, Persuasion +4, Performance +4
- **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 13
- **Gnome Cunning.** Advantage on Int, Wis and Cha saves against magic.
- **Wind Veil.** Ranged attack rolls against Obby have disadvantage while he
  is conscious and unrestrained.
- **Held Breath.** Obby does not need to breathe. Stolen Breath and Pocket Calm
  effects do not impair him.

**Actions**
- **Multiattack.** Two Gale Lance attacks.
- **Gale Lance.** *Ranged Spell Attack:* +6, range 60 ft., one target.
  *Hit:* 10 (2d6+3) bludgeoning, and the target is pushed 5 feet.
- **Stolen Breath (Recharge 5-6).** 30-foot cone. DC 14 Constitution save.
  *Fail:* 22 (5d8) force damage, and the target cannot speak or cast spells with
  verbal components until the end of its next turn. *Success:* half damage only.
- **Pocket Calm (1/Day).** A 20-foot-radius sphere centred on a point within
  60 feet, per [the race's Borrowed Breath](../races/hushbound-gnomes.md#racial-trait),
  lasting 1 minute and held with concentration (no spoken spells inside).

**Reactions**
- **Exhale.** When a creature within 5 feet hits him with a melee attack, it must
  make a DC 14 Strength save or be pushed 10 feet.

**What ends a fight without a kill.** At 13 hit points or fewer, a camera
turned on him, a fair offer of terms, or an applauding audience. Any of the
three.
