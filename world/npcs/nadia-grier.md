# Nadia Grier

## Description

Quiet, patient, and entirely comfortable being the person nobody in the
room is looking at — [Silas Renner](silas-renner.md)'s subordinate, not
his equal, and she's never once resented the difference. Posted as
forward security near wherever Meridian Peak is actually holding ground,
she doesn't engage unless she already has an angle and an exit. Carries a
compound-issue rifle and a merge-warped line-launcher, the same "pre-merge
tech gone strange" register as Renner's own gear.

## What the Party Knows

The party has been shot at, once, from a distance, by someone they never
saw. **Her identity is not resolved in the fiction** — this file exists
for the DM's use; nothing here should reach the players until they
actually identify her at the table.

## DM Only

**Session 3, resolved.** She's the shooter — see
[Session 3](../../sessions/03-the-flooded-crown.md). Fired on
[Phil Bernard](../people/phil-bernard.md) from the direction of the
Medical Supplies Factory during the corrupted-puma fight at the Toy
Store, which tracks cleanly: she was already posted there as Meridian
Peak's forward security for [the Gamma Vault](../../encounters/the-color-game.md)
long before the party ever pushed that node into a fight. The shot
landing as a graze rather than a kill was the actual roll at the table,
not a mechanical guarantee — she is not pulling punches.

**Why she took the shot.** Not ordered to, necessarily — Renner's style is
patient and controlled, and an unprompted regicide attempt on a rival
fragment's King is a real risk if it's traced back to Meridian Peak. Play
it as Grier's own initiative: she had the angle, she took it, and whether
Marsh or Renner actually sanctioned it afterward is a good loose thread
if the party ever gets her identity and pressures Meridian Peak over it.

**What she'd do under pressure.** Never closes to melee by choice — see
her Grapple Retreat, below. If cornered with the ability unavailable, she
fights defensively and looks for literally any opening to disengage rather
than trade blows; she has no interest in a fair fight and doesn't consider
retreating a failure.

**Statblock.**

*Medium humanoid (human), lawful evil*

- **Armor Class** 15 (reinforced tactical gear)
- **Hit Points** 27 (5d8 + 5)
- **Speed** 30 ft.
- **STR** 10 (+0) **DEX** 16 (+3) **CON** 12 (+1) **INT** 12 (+1) **WIS** 14 (+2) **CHA** 10 (+0)
- **Skills** Perception +4, Stealth +5
- **Senses** passive Perception 14
- **Languages** Common
- **Challenge** 2 (450 XP) — deliberately weaker than Renner's CR 4; a
  serious skirmish threat, not a boss.

**Sniper.** Grier doesn't have disadvantage on ranged attack rolls made at
long range.

**Ambush Discipline.** If Grier hasn't taken damage since the start of her
last turn, the first time she hits with her rifle each round she deals an
extra 2d6 damage.

**Grapple Retreat (Recharge 5-6).** When a creature Grier can see moves
within 5 feet of her, she can use her reaction to fire her line-launcher
and yank herself up to 30 feet away, to an unoccupied space she can see.
This movement doesn't provoke opportunity attacks. She has no answer for
melee without this — see What She'd Do Under Pressure, above.

*Actions*

**Suppressed Rifle.** *Ranged Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, range 80/320 ft.,
one target. *Hit:* 7 (1d10 + 2) piercing damage (plus Ambush Discipline,
if it applies).

**Sidearm.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +2 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.
*Hit:* 2 (1d4) piercing damage. Her fallback only — she'd rather retreat
than use this.

**Playing her long-term.** A believable recurring threat if the party ever
crosses Meridian Peak again — competent enough to be dangerous, junior
enough to Renner that she's not the face of the faction, which makes her a
good "the danger isn't over just because we beat the boss" beat for a
later session.
