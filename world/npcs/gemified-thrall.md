# Gemified Thrall

The local victims of the duergar's gem-feeding operation: naked humanoids with red gems pushed through their skin, hostile to anyone ungemified. Details in [Fearus Dungeon](../places/fearus-dungeon.md).

See stat block in [The Underspill](../../encounters/the-underspill.md) (Combat Notes).
