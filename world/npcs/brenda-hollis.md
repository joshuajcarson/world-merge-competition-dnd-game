# Brenda Hollis

## Description

Contract security, not mall security, and she will correct you on that
distinction unprompted. Centurion Protective Services sent her to cover the
"As Seen On TV" grand opening because a name like Suzanne Somers apparently
justifies the line item. Laminated badge, a baton she's never had reason to
use, and a genuinely sharp eye for a room that she spends almost no effort
acting on. See [Private Security Log — Briarwood Mall](../lore/private-security-log-briarwood-mall.md)
for her voice at length.

## What the Party Knows

Nothing yet — she's a face in a Centurion polo somewhere near the fountain
court, indistinguishable to the party from any other staff working the event.

## DM Only

Whether she survives the merge, and whether she's worth promoting to a
recurring NPC afterward, is open — same open-question treatment as
[Suzanne Somers](suzanne-somers.md). Her log gives a DM a second, independent
pre-merge witness to the mall's closet-count problem, which is useful if the
party ever finds it and wants corroboration that it isn't just their own
memory playing tricks. Her attitude (lazy, observant, unwilling to be the one
who reports anything) is the character hook if she comes back — someone who
noticed the world was already wrong and said nothing is a good source of
guilt, denial, or grim vindication once the merge actually happens.
