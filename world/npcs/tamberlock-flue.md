# Tamberlock "Flue" Gearwhistle

## Description

Goggled, soot-faced, and always tapping a brass pressure gauge with a fingernail
as though it might change its mind. Flue is the only gnome in the Concord who
talks fast, because she spends the least breath on her own sentences and the most
on her work. She calls every machine "she" and the Organ "my girl."

## What the Party Knows

Nothing yet. First contact is
[The Demerit Comes Due](../../encounters/the-demerit-comes-due.md).

## DM Only

**Motive.** The Gale Organ is hers. She does not much care who wins as long as the
Organ gets to sing at full pressure at least once on camera. That is her
weakness and the party's lever: a tinker who wants a clean, impressive discharge
will argue over placement of the discharge, and can be distracted, flattered, or
hired by someone who respects the machine. Jack Mercer is the natural counterpart.

**Statblock.** Small humanoid, lawful neutral. About CR 2 (450 XP).

- **AC** 14 · **HP** 38 (7d6+14) · **Speed** 25 ft.
- STR 9 DEX 14 CON 14 INT 16 WIS 11 CHA 9
- **Gnome Cunning.** Advantage on Int, Wis and Cha saves against magic.
- **Machine Sense.** She knows the Organ's exact Pressure at all times and notices
  tampering automatically.
- **Actions.** *Spanner:* +4, 1d6+2 bludgeoning. *Valve Pistol* (ranged +4, 30/90 ft.,
  1d8+2 bludgeoning, pushed 5 ft.).
- **Bonus Action — Crank.** Adds 1 Pressure to the [Gale Organ](../../encounters/the-demerit-comes-due.md#node-d--the-parking-lot)
  while she is within 5 feet of it.
- **Reaction — Reroute.** When the Organ would discharge, she redirects its line
  by up to 45 degrees.

**What ends a fight without a kill.** A disabled or destroyed Organ and an
audience. She will bargain for the wreck.
