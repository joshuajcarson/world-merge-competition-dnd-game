# Priya Anand

## Description

Overnight-shift air-traffic controller before the merge; the discipline that job
demanded is most of why she's still lucid. Where other Fulgurites talk in
fragments, Priya still gives orders in clipped, procedural phrasing — headings,
distances, "traffic," directions barked at people who have no idea what any of
it means — because that is still, underneath everything, how her brain organizes
a crisis. Scar tissue at her temples and down both forearms, glass-smooth,
faintly warm to the touch. She holds a knot of Fulgurite bands together the way
she used to hold a sector of airspace together: by refusing, out loud, to let it
become unmanageable.

## DM Only

**Why she's different.** [Overcharge](../races/fulgurites.md) hit her too, the
same as every other Fulgurite, but whatever combination of temperament and luck
kept her mind mostly intact also gave her something none of the others have:
she can read the charge building in someone else before it goes off, and she
can pull the trigger herself.

**Conduct.** As an action, Priya can force the [Overcharge trait](../races/fulgurites.md)
of one other Fulgurite she can see within 30 feet to trigger immediately, even
if that Fulgurite is above half HP — the target still rolls or chooses the
effect (force surge, psychic scream, or arc-light) as normal. This costs the
target its own Overcharge for the rest of the encounter the same as if it had
triggered naturally; Priya isn't giving them a free extra discharge, she's
choosing *when* the one they already have goes off. Used well, this turns a
band of ordinary Fulgurites from a scattered, reactive mob into something that
opens a fight with three simultaneous blasts on her initiative count instead of
however combat happens to unfold.

**Her own charge.** She still has a personal Overcharge and it still triggers
at half HP same as anyone's — she has not escaped what she is, she has just
learned to point it at other people first. Whether she's ever consciously
grappled with that, or whether leadership is the thing she's using not to think
about it, is a good character beat to play, not resolve.

**Playing her.** Not a monster, not a villain — a competent person doing triage
on an impossible situation with tools that were never meant for this. She will
try to talk before she orders an attack, and she is genuinely, visibly trying
to keep her people from dying pointlessly, including against a party that
hasn't given her a reason to see them as a threat yet. That doesn't mean she
won't Conduct three Overcharges into a group that corners her band with weapons
drawn — it means she'd rather not have to.

**Statblock notes.** No tier fixed on purpose — see [Fulgurites](../races/fulgurites.md)'s
open question on challenge band. Build her as a controller/leader reskin
(a spellcaster-adjacent base with Conduct standing in for a spell) one solid
step tougher than the ordinary Fulgurites she leads, not a solo boss herself —
her danger is what she does to the fight's economy, not her own melee presence.
