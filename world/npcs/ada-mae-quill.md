# Ada Mae Quill

## Description

A gaunt, antlered, long-eared woman in a faded gingham dress and a man's boots,
with a scarf over her nose and mouth that she only lowers to say something
that matters. Fifty-ish and tireless. She says "ma'am" and "sir" to everyone
including the Pylon.

## What the Party Knows

Nothing yet.

## DM Only

Stat her as a **Veteran** (about CR 3) with Jackalope Dash (bonus-action Dash),
and give her a hunting rifle (ranged +5, range 80/320, 2d8+3 piercing).
She will offer the party the **derby** before anything else and will not
cheat at it. She expects the same.
