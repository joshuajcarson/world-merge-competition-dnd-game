# Gildrot

## Description

Bigger than the rest of its Luck by maybe a head, and the only one whose
Doombow isn't just found wood — this one's grown into a shape that reads,
almost, as deliberately made. Doesn't lead by giving orders so much as by
being first to whatever's shiniest, which the rest of the band reads as
authority because it's never once been wrong about where the gold is.

## What the Party Knows

Nothing yet — first contact is
[The Golden Goose Chase](../../encounters/the-golden-goose-chase.md).

## DM Only

**Not smarter, just luckier — and it knows it.** Gildrot isn't a tactician;
it's the one Leprechaun whose Gold Hunger has never led it wrong, which in
a species this opportunistic passes for genius. Don't play it as clever —
play it as supernaturally, infuriatingly lucky, which is scarier at the
table because the party can't out-think something that isn't thinking.

**Statblock.** Reskin a **Goblin Boss** (CR 1) with
[Aether Leprechauns](../races/aether-leprechauns.md)' Gold Hunger and
Ravenous Bite traits layered on, plus:

**Doombow.** *Ranged Weapon Attack:* +4 to hit, range 60/240 ft., one
target. *Hit:* 7 (2d6) necrotic damage, and the target's hit point maximum
is reduced by an amount equal to the necrotic damage taken. This reduction
lasts until the target finishes a long rest.

**What ends a fight without a kill.** Gildrot breaks off the instant a
larger, closer pile of gold appears anywhere in sight — a thrown coin
purse is a legitimate way to end this fight without anyone landing a
killing blow. It does not have pride to wound; it has priorities, and gold
always outranks a grudge.

**Token Prompt.** Campaign default art style is Glitch-Corrupted Render —
see `.claude/skills/campaign-chronicle/references/monster-art-style.md`.
The Doombow stays as a combat mechanic above, but leave it out of the art;
the sack reads better visually and still sells Gold Hunger.
