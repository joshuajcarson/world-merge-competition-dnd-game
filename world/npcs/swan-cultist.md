# Swan Cultist

## Description

An orc in an ill-fitting coat of mixed feathers: swan, goose, duck, once a pigeon, and none of the cultists
could tell you which is which. Holds a stone-headed axe like a feather duster. Utterly sincere about beauty.

## What the Party Knows

Fought the cult's dancers around the fire in [Session 7](../../sessions/07-the-golden-goose.md); one cultist from that
fight lives at the mall now.

## DM Only

Part of the [Cult of the Swan](../factions/cult-of-the-swan.md). Play it straight.

### Swan Cultist (CR 1/4, 50 XP)

*Medium humanoid (orc), chaotic neutral.* **AC** 12 (feather coat) · **HP** 15 (2d8+6) · **Speed** 30 ft.
STR 15 (+2) DEX 11 (+0) CON 16 (+3) INT 7 (-2) WIS 10 (+0) CHA 9 (-1).
**Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 10. **Languages** Common, Orc.
- **Aggressive.** As a bonus action, it moves up to its speed toward a hostile creature it can see.
- **Plumage.** Disadvantage on Stealth checks. Advantage on saves against being frightened while it is within
  30 feet of Vainhusk.
- **Feather Axe.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 7 (1d10+2) slashing damage.
- **Pylon Claimant.** If it is the only creature within 5 feet of the Pylon, it can touch the Pylon with a bonus
  action (no check) to start the Cult's count.
