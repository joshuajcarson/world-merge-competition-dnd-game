# Sister Maerwyn

## Description

The youngest of Eldath's novices at the chantry, and the one standing
closest to the reflecting pool — mid-transformation, human sliding into
swan — when the merge hit. She never finished shifting either way. What
surfaces at the bottom of the pool is something arrested: too much wing to
pass for a person, too much grief and prayer still audible in her cry to
pass for an animal. She never leaves the water. She doesn't have to — every
other Swanfolk in [the Drowned Chantry](../places/drowned-chantry.md)
answers her cry before their own instincts.

## What the Party Knows

Nothing yet — she hasn't been encountered.

## DM Only

**Function.** The dungeon's boss encounter and the mechanical source of
[Swanfolk](../races/swanfolk.md)'s Reed-Song — treat every other Swanfolk in
the chantry as reinforcing her cry rather than casting their own
independent version of it, which makes silencing or calming her the actual
win condition for the Reflecting Pool room, not just killing the last
Swanfolk standing. Run her as a solo-boss-tier statblock: full Racial Trait
kit (Water's Grace, Reed-Song at will rather than once per short rest,
Wingstorm on a bloodied trigger like any other Swanfolk) plus whatever
tier-appropriate spellcasting the table needs to make her a genuine
capstone — *entangle*, *shape water*, and a swan-dive charge attack all fit
the flavor without reaching outside a druid/nature spell list.

**Tone.** She is not evil and barely qualifies as hostile in the way a
monster is hostile — she is a person locked mid-transformation, still
praying, and the fight against her should feel like a tragedy the party can
choose to interrupt instead of finish. A party that recognizes what she is
and tries to talk, calm, or ritual-cleanse her instead of killing her is
playing this encounter exactly as intended; see
[Swanfolk](../races/swanfolk.md)'s open question on whether that's
recoverable.

**Open question:** what, if anything, is actually recoverable at the bottom
of that pool — Eldath's own attention, a fragment of the original ritual, a
plain body under the feathers — is deliberately unset. Decide only if a
table pursues it that far.

See [Swan Song](../../encounters/swan-song.md) Node F for her staged
encounter, including the calm-her-down approach that keeps this question
open versus the ending where it doesn't.
