# Lazare

## Description

An elf from [Scornubel](../places/scornubel.md), first seen by the party
through the mall's windows as a stranger arriving out of the "Grasslands
City" — their own name for the river city on the horizon before they learned
its real one. Came to the mall alone, looking for whoever was in charge, and
found [Wade Kessler](wade-kessler.md), who introduced him to
[Phil Bernard](../../sessions/01-mall-rats-and-the-grasslands-city.md).

## What the Party Knows

His city has been ravaged by the armies of Tiamat, and he came to trade and
ask for help. He was visibly, sincerely impressed by Briarwood's indoor
plumbing — running water, a self-flushing privy — and the party struck a deal
letting Scornubel's people use it for a silver piece a head. He later led the
party to the mercenary district to investigate the ninja attack, and from
there to a refugee camp at a geyser: [the Hollow Spring](../factions/the-hollow-spring.md).

## DM Only

**Supersedes the original first-contact plan.** [Halric Ammakyl](halric-ammakyl.md)
was drafted as Scornubel's first-contact figure before any session was
played; the table used Lazare instead. Halric's file is left as-is rather
than deleted — he's still a name available to use for a different Council
figure if one is needed, but he did not play the first-contact role in
actual continuity. Don't reintroduce him as if he already met the party.

**Role going forward.** Lazare reads as genuinely trying to build a working
relationship between Scornubel and the mall — the plumbing deal, the warning
about the Stewies (see [The Stewies](../factions/the-stewies.md)), and the
introduction to the Hollow Spring were all him actively helping rather than
extracting value. Whether that's purely personal goodwill or something he's
doing on behalf of a faction back home (the [Coster Council](../factions/coster-council.md)?
someone else?) is open — worth deciding if the party starts wondering who he
actually answers to.

**Name note:** referred to once as "Lazeral" in the raw session log — same
person, treat as the same spelling inconsistency rather than a second
character, unless the table's memory says otherwise.
