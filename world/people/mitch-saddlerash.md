# Mitch Saddlerash

## Description

A mall performer working the grand-opening crowd as "Mr. Mime, Jr." the
night of the merge. Came through as an aasimar. The party's default
negotiator and organizer — the one who talks a panicking crowd into
barricading windows instead of scattering, who closes a deal, who gets a
refugee camp fed and sheltered before anyone else has finished reacting to
it existing.

## Story So Far

Session 0: working the ThighMaster line as "Mr. Mime, Jr." when the merge
hit. See [Session 0](../../sessions/00-welcome-to-the-world-merge-competition.md).

Session 1: came through the merge an aasimar. Talked the panicking
post-merge crowd into barricading windows instead of scattering. Closed the
deal with [Ironquill](../npcs/ironquill.md) that freed twenty ratkin from
[Sector 7](../places/cohort-15-holding-sector.md) in exchange for his
fealty, then spent the rest of the day integrating them into Briarwood's
community. See [Session 1](../../sessions/01-mall-rats-and-the-grasslands-city.md).

Session 2: got Briarwood's volunteer militia off the ground. Organized
squirrel-hunting parties to help with food. Offered Scornubel's refugees
discounted mall access in exchange for pledging support to the
[Way of the Gander](../factions/way-of-the-gander.md) and to Briarwood
itself — the deal that's driving the mall's growing outer commune. One of
[the Hollow Spring](../factions/the-hollow-spring.md)'s elders took a real
liking to him. Later granted [Suzanne Somers](../npcs/suzanne-somers.md) her
annulment through the Gander in exchange for her formally abdicating the
Mall Queen title. See [Session 2](../../sessions/02-the-mall-king.md).

## DM Only

**Does most of the Way of the Gander's actual organizing.**
[Sesug Tsirch](sesug-tsirch.md) is the religion's central figure and
miracle-worker; Mitch is the one turning that into a functioning admission
economy and refugee pipeline for Briarwood. Worth keeping that division of
labor visible — the Gander's real-world weight is coming from Mitch's
negotiating as much as Sesug's miracles.
