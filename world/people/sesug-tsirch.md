# Sesug Tsirch

## Description

Visiting Briarwood Mall's grand opening with a community group, off his
medication, and having a rough night of it when the merge hit. Came through
as a humanoid goose — the table immediately started calling him "Geezus,"
and it stuck hard enough that it's now the name of a religion. Earnest and
strange in a way that reads as sincere rather than a bit, which is most of
why people keep believing him.

## Story So Far

Session 0: near the ThighMaster signing table, panicking, when
[Phil Bernard](phil-bernard.md) "accidentally" tackled him off the
second-story balcony to calm him down — neither of them was hurt by the
fall, which nobody could explain. See
[Session 0](../../sessions/00-welcome-to-the-world-merge-competition.md).

Session 1: came through the merge a humanoid goose, immediately nicknamed
"Geezus" at the table. Worked mall security alongside
[Phil Bernard](phil-bernard.md) once the first day's chaos settled. See
[Session 1](../../sessions/01-mall-rats-and-the-grasslands-city.md).

Session 2: fed an entire refugee crowd with what looked like one cinnamon
roll and a slice of jerky — a miracle beat that started as a joke and has
kept real narrative weight since. Washed an elder's feet at
[the Hollow Spring](../factions/the-hollow-spring.md)'s geyser camp as a
courtesy and was given [Hallowspring's Thanks](../../items/hallowsprings-thanks.md)
in return. Kept using his earrings to feed the growing crowd outside the
mall. As the cult around him kept growing, went with the party into the
flooding arcade dungeon and showed the giant swan guarding it the Gander's
love instead of a fight — it fled rather than die for the fight. See
[Session 2](../../sessions/02-the-mall-king.md).

## DM Only

**The Way of the Gander's actual miracle-worker.** Sesug is the one
generating the beats that read as real to the refugees converting —
feeding a crowd, turning a boss fight into a non-fight by showing up as
"the Gander's love." [Mitch Saddlerash](mitch-saddlerash.md) does the
organizing that turns that into Briarwood's admission economy; keep both
halves visible rather than letting one absorb the other.

**Off his medication, pre-merge.** Session 0 establishes this about his
human self; hasn't come up again since the transformation and doesn't need
to unless the table brings it back — a detail to have on hand, not a thread
to force.
