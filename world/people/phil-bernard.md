# Phil Bernard

## Description

Fresh out of high school and living his literal dream job as a Briarwood
Mall security guard the night of the grand opening. When the merge hit, he
came through as a half-dwarf. Takes the mall's defense personally — the
first to reach for a keyring, the first to organize a fight, the first to
put himself between the crowd and whatever's coming through a window.

## Story So Far

Session 0: tackled a panicking [Sesug Tsirch](sesug-tsirch.md) off the
second-story balcony to calm him down — neither of them was hurt by the
fall, which nobody could explain even before the merge. Cornered
[Dale Pruitt](../npcs/dale-pruitt.md) and [Renee Castillo](../npcs/renee-castillo.md)
moments before the merge fired. See
[Session 0](../../sessions/00-welcome-to-the-world-merge-competition.md).

Session 1: came through the merge a half-dwarf. Talked Dale and Renee down
without a fight and confiscated Renee's transformed drill. Fought off the
purple aether-wolf tearing through JCPenney alongside
[Jack Mercer](jack-mercer.md) at [Wade Kessler](../npcs/wade-kessler.md)'s
request. Found the [Mall Security Keyring](../../items/mall-security-keyring.md)
on the ground after the mall's first move. Helped clear
[Sector 7](../places/cohort-15-holding-sector.md) alongside the rest of the
party. Spent the rest of the day working mall security with
[Geezus](sesug-tsirch.md). See
[Session 1](../../sessions/01-mall-rats-and-the-grasslands-city.md).

Session 2: trained the mall's new volunteer militia into something
functional. Bought splint mail and defense gear for them in Scornubel.
Escorted [Suzanne Somers](../npcs/suzanne-somers.md) as she left to fight a
duergar queen and, on her return charged with power the table read as a
real challenge to the party's own authority, married her — becoming Mall
King. The marriage was annulled the same session once Suzanne recalled she
was still married to Alan Hamel back on Earth, but the title stuck: Phil
Bernard is Briarwood's one and only Mall King. See
[Session 2](../../sessions/02-the-mall-king.md).

Session 3: worked at actually being a good king rather than just holding
the title, and started winning over skeptical Briarwood residents one at a
time. During [the Color Game](../../encounters/the-color-game.md), took a
sniper shot from an assailant still unidentified — walked away from it, but
the shooter's identity is an open thread. See
[Session 3](../../sessions/03-the-flooded-crown.md).

## DM Only

**Mall King, structurally undefined.** What the title actually carries in
terms of authority over the mall's governance hasn't been decided — see
[Briarwood Mall](../places/briarwood-mall.md)'s DM notes. Worth deciding
deliberately rather than leaving it purely comedic, since Phil is the one
holding it.

**The unexplained soft landing.** Phil and Sesug's Session 0 fall off the
balcony was unscripted — an early, unexplained bleed of the merge's physics
rather than anything Phil himself did on purpose. See
[Session 0](../../sessions/00-welcome-to-the-world-merge-competition.md)'s
DM notes. Worth deciding whether this kind of thing recurs around him
specifically or was a one-off tied to the merge's timing.

**The sniper, resolved.** [Nadia Grier](../npcs/nadia-grier.md), Meridian
Peak's forward scout — see her file. She was already posted near the
Medical Supplies Factory as security for [the Gamma Vault](../../encounters/the-color-game.md)
before the party ever pushed that node into a fight, which is consistent
with Marsh and Renner themselves still not being physically present at the
Game Floor for Session 3 — an embedded scout isn't the same as Meridian
Peak's leadership showing up. **Her identity stays unresolved in the
fiction** until the party actually identifies her at the table; this note
is DM-only.
