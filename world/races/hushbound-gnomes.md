# Hushbound Gnomes

## Description

Gnomes from a different Faerûn than the one the party's merge brought in. In
theirs, no dragon cult won. [Yan-C-Bin](../factions/the-hush-concord.md), the
Elemental Prince of Evil Air, did, and the first thing he took was the wind. No
breeze has crossed that world in living memory. Sails hang dead, flags hang
stiff, and the whole sky sits still, an event the survivors simply call **the
Calm**. All of the world's moving air now belongs to the Prince, and he lends it
back to a few thousand gnomes in exchange for a **tithe of breath**: one
held breath a day, given up on purpose, which goes into a brass-and-hide
bladder-pack they wear like a second ribcage.

The tithe works as a trade, and the gnomes believe in it. The air they are
lent will carry a stone wall off its footings; the price is that every one of
them lives slightly short of breath, a faint permanent wheeze they have turned
into manners (soft voices, shallow bows, a habit of finishing each other's
sentences so nobody has to spend a whole breath on them). They are tinkerers by
nature, so the "lungs" are well engineered: pressure gauges, relief valves,
stamped serial numbers.

They are not new to the broadcast. They came out of an earlier cohort as
survivors and as crowd favorites. See [Crowd Favorites](../lore/crowd-favorites.md).

## Racial Trait

**Borrowed Breath.** Every Hushbound gnome wears a **lung**: a pressurised pack of
the Prince's air. Once per short rest a gnome can open its lung as a bonus
action to do one of the following. Pick the effect to suit the statblock you are
reskinning.
1. **Gale Shove.** One creature within 15 feet makes a DC 12 Strength save or is
   pushed 15 feet and knocked prone.
2. **Stolen Breath.** One creature within 20 feet makes a DC 12 Constitution
   save or cannot speak or cast spells with verbal components until the end of
   its next turn, as the air leaves its lungs in one pull.
3. **Pocket Calm.** A 10-foot-radius sphere around a point within 30 feet goes
   completely still and silent until the start of the gnome's next turn. No sound
   passes in or out, gas and smoke are dispelled, and non-magical flame inside
   it goes out.

A gnome that has spent its lung is visibly deflated and short of breath: it has
disadvantage on Strength checks and cannot take the Dash action until it
finishes a short rest. The lungs are removable, and a lung taken from a gnome
who is out of the fight still holds the Prince's air for a creature that can
work the valves (DC 15 Tinker's Tools, one use).

**Gnome Cunning.** Advantage on Intelligence, Wisdom and Charisma saving
throws against magic.

**Reskinning note:** layer these onto a Bandit or Goblin base for rank and file,
a Bandit Captain or Mage for leaders. The named examples carry their own
blocks.

## DM Only

**What makes them dangerous is the quiet, not the volume.** The gnomes fight in
hushes: a [Pocket Calm](#racial-trait) over the party's casters, a wall of
muffled air in front of the Organ, a battlefield that goes silent a second
before it goes loud. Narrate the sound dropping out. Players should learn to
dread the moment their own footsteps stop.

**Why they are so polite.** They are on camera and they know it. They were
crowd favorites before they were soldiers, and their manners are a performance
that hardened into habit. They would rather win a Pylon than a body count (the
broadcast rewards the first and tires of the second), which gives the table a
real lever: a gnome that thinks the cameras are on it can be made to behave.

**The air is not Aether.** Their power comes from the Prince, not from ambient
Aether, which is almost certainly why they outlasted the last cohort that
should have eaten them. See [The Aether Hunger](../lore/the-aether-hunger.md).
Nobody involved, including the gnomes, knows that. Do not say it at the table.
