# Sextonbacks

## Description

Huge, slow, and gentle, the Aether World's undertakers. A Sextonback is the size
of a delivery truck, built like a giant sloth that lost an argument with a
cathedral: a long, drooping, blunt-toothed face; forelegs like felled trees;
and along its spine a ridge of tall bone plates strung with hollow, hanging
**bone-chimes** that toll as it walks. They eat the dead and almost nothing
else, and in a world of apex predators that is a full-time job. Where the Aether
World's big killers fight, a Sextonback follows a day or two behind, ringing.

They do not hunt, defend territory, or care about anything alive. They are
**passive**. A Sextonback will let a person walk under its belly. They are
nonetheless worth fearing, because they are unbelievably big, because they
arrive on the sound of a fight and never leave until it is clean, and because
whatever dies near one is gone within a minute or two, gear and all.

## Racial Trait

**Last Rites.** A Sextonback always knows the location of any dead creature
within a mile. On its turn, if it is within 5 feet of a corpse, it spends its
action to **eat it**: the body is destroyed, and anything the corpse carried is
swallowed. Feeding takes one round for a Medium or smaller creature, two for
Large, four for Huge. A body that has been eaten cannot be raised, revived or
questioned by any means short of a *wish*. Swallowed items are in the beast's
stomach and can be recovered only from its remains.

**Docile.** A Sextonback does not attack unless it takes damage, or something is
taken out of its mouth or from under its muzzle. Provoked, it fights hard but
sensibly, and calms again as soon as the provocation is gone and there is a
corpse to eat.

**Toll.** While it feeds or is startled, its bone-chimes ring, audible out to
300 feet. A DC 10 Perception check hears it coming over the noise of a fight.

## DM Only

**Why Agent loves them.** A battlefield that tidies itself is free television:
the bodies are gone before the next ad break, nothing needs to be explained, and
the beast is huge and unbothered. Viewers voted Deacon "Best Supporting Creature" in
a previous cohort's coverage. Hurting a Sextonback on camera costs the party
ratings, and Agent will make that clear.

**The tension to play.** Let the table feel the clock. If a gnome falls, he is
dead and the Sextonback is coming, and a PC at 0 hit points is not a corpse
and is safe, but a dead PC is gone. **Optional: soften this** by having the
Sextonback ignore anyone wearing a contestant tag. Decide before the first
session of play and tell the table honestly.

**Uses, if the party gets clever.** The Sextonback is a weapon you cannot aim:
bait lures it (a corpse or fresh meat; DC 15 Animal Handling to steer it), its body
is full cover and climbable high ground (Athletics DC 10), and the toll can mask
other noises or cover a retreat. An enemy that is trying to loot bodies finds the
bodies gone.

**Statblock — Sextonback (adult).** Huge beast, unaligned. About CR 6 (2,300 XP).

- **AC** 16 (natural armor, bone plates) · **HP** 168 (16d12+64) · **Speed** 20 ft.
- STR 22 DEX 6 CON 18 INT 2 WIS 11 CHA 5
- **Senses** blindsight 30 ft., passive Perception 10
- **Last Rites, Docile, Toll** as above.
- **Bulwark.** Its flank provides total cover to creatures behind it.
- **Slow Mass.** It moves through other creatures' spaces as if they were difficult
  terrain, but it cannot end its move in one.
- **Actions (provoked only).** **Multiattack:** one Slam and one Trample.
  **Slam:** +9, reach 10 ft., 2d10+6 bludgeoning. **Trample:** one Large or
  smaller prone creature in its reach, DC 16 Strength save, 3d8+6 bludgeoning, or
  half on a success.
- **Reaction — Mournful Bellow.** When it first takes damage, every creature within
  30 feet makes a DC 14 Wisdom save or is frightened until the end of its next turn.
