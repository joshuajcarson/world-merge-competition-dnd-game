# Aether Wolves

## Description

Large, purple, wolf-shaped pack hunters from the Aether World. The coat sheds a faint
violet glow, brightest when the animal is hurt or angry. One burst through the JCPenney
windows in [Session 1](../../sessions/01-mall-rats-and-the-grasslands-city.md) and killed
people before the party stopped it. They hunt in packs of four to six under a larger
alpha, and they follow the scent of fear.

## Racial Trait

**Aether Glow.** The coat sheds dim violet light out to 10 feet. When an Aether Wolf is
first bloodied, the glow flares; an **alpha** also discharges a burst of raw aether (see
[the Alpha](../npcs/alpha-aether-wolf.md)). **Pack Tactics:** advantage on an attack roll
against a creature if an ally of the wolf is within 5 feet of it and isn't incapacitated.

Statblocks (wolf, alpha and pup) are in
[Sweethearts of Sears](../../encounters/sweethearts-of-sears.md#combat-notes).

## Notable Examples

- [The Alpha](../npcs/alpha-aether-wolf.md) — the pack leader released at the Sweethearts of
  Sears pregame, brightest coat in the pack.
- The pack (wolves and pups) need no names.

## What the Party Knows

They have fought one before: the purple wolf-shaped predator in JCPenney, Session 1.
