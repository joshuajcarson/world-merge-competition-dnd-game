# Aether Hornbacks

## Description

Dinosaur-sized plains grazers from the Aether World: horse-to-rhino size, with a bony frill, a pair of forward-curved
brow horns, and a spine of armor plates that sheds a faint violet shimmer. They travel in herds of ten to thirty
under a heavy **bull**, graze on whatever the Aether World grows, and do not start fights. They watch the herd, and
the herd watches the biggest, brightest thing in sight.

The [Cult of the Swan](../factions/cult-of-the-swan.md) has found out that a Hornback follows the most beautiful
bird it can see. It is a harmless habit that the Cult has turned into a weapon.

## Racial Trait

**Herd Mind.** A Hornback follows whatever the herd is following. If the herd is led by a bright, tall standard
(like the Cult's Plume), it is **Lured** while it can see the standard and moves toward and attacks the nearest
non-cultist. A Hornback that loses sight of the lure gets a DC 12 Wisdom save at the start of its turn to snap out
of it; on a success it stops and grazes, and on a failure it flees (frightened) for 1 round.

**Charge.** If a Hornback moves at least 20 feet straight at a target and then hits with Gore, the target takes an
extra 2d6 piercing damage and must succeed on a DC 13 Strength save or be knocked prone.
