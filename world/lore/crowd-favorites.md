# Crowd Favorites

## Overview

In the second act of [the Blood Bowl](the-blood-bowl.md), [Agent](../npcs/agent.md)
reopens the roster. Survivors of earlier World Merge cohorts (the contestants the
audience liked best, each with a devoted following) are brought back in as new
factions and species, dropped into the merged world with their own territory
claims. They fill the board with new faces for the first time since Session 0.

## What the Party Knows

Nothing yet.

## DM Only

**Who they are.** Teams that survived their own cohort and were kept on file
because they test well: the [Hush Concord](../factions/the-hush-concord.md) (gnomes
from a Faerûn the Prince of Evil Air won),
the [Black Sunday Kin](../factions/black-sunday-kin.md) (dust-bowl jackalope-folk), the
[Dartwood Court](../factions/dartwood-court.md) (rainforest poison-frog court).
More are available; the rule is one short tag line and one visual hook each.

**How Agent uses them.** They are scripted rivals, introduced with a recap reel
and a catchphrase, and Agent plays favorites with them the way it did with Meridian
Peak. A crowd favorite that gets a mean matchup is a ratings win; one that is
eliminated by an underdog is *better*.

**What they are not.** They are not Cohort 23's own contestants and they do not
share its mysteries. They are veterans, they are good at being watched, and they
have seen what happens to cohorts. They are all slightly scared of Agent, and none of them
will say so on camera.

**The Aether Hunger.** Every previous cohort turned violent from the inside out
(see [The Aether Hunger](the-aether-hunger.md)). These survivors survived
something, and nobody has asked what. A good, slow thread: a crowd favorite who
privately knows what "getting sick" looked like in their cohort.

**Design rule for adding more.** A crowd favorite is a **faction** (a place and
a want), a **species** or tweak (a one-line trait), and a **way to settle it
without a war** (derby, performance, bargain). Keep the three. It is what makes
Act Two a game and not a trudge.
