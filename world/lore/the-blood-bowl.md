# The Blood Bowl

## Overview

Announced by [Agent](../npcs/agent.md), mid-Session 3, with a fixed
countdown: four days out as of that announcement, with "cross fragment
violence... encouraged" in the meantime. No further mechanical or narrative
detail has been established yet.

## What the Party Knows

Exactly what Agent told them: it's coming, it's soon, and Agent isn't
discouraging conflict between merge fragments before it happens.

## DM Only

**Genuinely undefined — decide deliberately, not by default.** Given Agent's
established character (drama-hungry, ratings-driven, controls fragment
timing), the Blood Bowl reads as a broadcast event Agent is engineering on
purpose — plausibly a forced confrontation between fragments (Briarwood vs.
Scornubel? something else entirely?), a gladiatorial spectacle, or both.
Whatever it turns out to be, it should feel like exactly the kind of thing
[Agent's file](../npcs/agent.md) describes: manufactured drama, not organic
conflict, with real stakes underneath the game-show framing.

**The clock is real.** "Four days" was stated in-fiction and should be
tracked as an actual countdown from the Session 3 announcement, not a vague
future threat — see that session's note for the exact in-fiction date it
was announced.

**Cross-fragment violence "encouraged."** Worth deciding what mechanical or
narrative teeth this has — does Agent actively reward PvP-adjacent conflict
between fragments before the Bowl, or is it just permission/color? Pick an
answer before it matters at the table.
