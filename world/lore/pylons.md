# Pylons

## Overview

Announced by [Agent](../npcs/agent.md) in [Session 8](../../sessions/08-pylons-and-moonshine.md):
each shard has a Pylon in its base. The rule of [The Blood Bowl](the-blood-bowl.md) is to protect
your own and steal other shards' Pylons. See the encounters
[The Demerit Comes Due](../../encounters/the-demerit-comes-due.md) and
[The Dust Flats](../../encounters/the-dust-flats.md) for how the DM has been running them.

## What the Party Knows

Protect yours, steal the others'. They defended Briarwood's against the Hush Concord gnomes in Session 8.

## DM Only

Per Agent's ad in Session 8, the competition exists to produce aether for the creator's society.
What a Pylon actually does with that is undecided; see [The Aether Hunger](the-aether-hunger.md).
