# The Hollow Spring

## Description

A band of refugee matriarchs camped at a geyser near
[Scornubel](../places/scornubel.md), worshipping water because their own
gods have gone silent since the coast fell. [Lazare](../npcs/lazare.md) led
the party to their camp while investigating the Stewies. One of the elders
took a liking to Mitch Saddlerash; when Sesug Tsirch (playing "Geezus")
washed an elder's feet as a courtesy, she gave him the **Gift of the Hollow
Spring** in thanks — see [Hallowspring's Thanks](../../items/hallowsprings-thanks.md),
whose provenance now traces to this group rather than the invented merchant
coster originally drafted for it.

## Goals

- Survive as refugees with no functioning temple and no god currently
  answering.
- Unclear beyond that — they had no information about the Council for the
  party, but responded warmly to the Way of the Gander's energy when it was
  described to them.

## Notable Members

*(None named yet — "one of the elders" is on record as fond of Mitch, but

## What the Party Knows

Met once, briefly, through Lazare. Warm, currently godless, and gave the
party's Sesug a real gift for a real courtesy — a good first impression with
room to develop into something more (a genuine alliance, converts to the
[Way of the Gander](way-of-the-gander.md), or a future favor owed).

## DM Only

**Naming coincidence, not the same as the item's original draft.**
[Hallowspring's Thanks](../../items/hallowsprings-thanks.md) was originally
written around an invented merchant, "Ostrander Vale of the Hallowspring
Coster," before this session's notes existed. The real Hollow Spring is a
refugee matriarchy, not a trading house, and has no connection to the
[Coster Council](coster-council.md). The item's file has been updated to
match what actually happened; Ostrander Vale no longer exists in continuity.

**Open thread:** whether "gods have gone silent" connects to anything about
Tiamat's actual post-merge fate is worth deciding only if the party goes
looking — see [Scornubel](../places/scornubel.md)'s own DM notes on that
question.
