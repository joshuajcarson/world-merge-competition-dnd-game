# The Way of the Gander

## Description

A religion that did not exist before Session 1 and now has converts —
founded around Sesug Tsirch, transformed by the merge into a humanoid goose
the table has taken to calling "Geezus," and Mitch Saddlerash, who does most
of the actual preaching. What started as in-character bit has picked up real
believers: refugees at the mall's growing outer commune pledge support to
the Gander as part of the deal for discounted access, and the
[Hollow Spring](the-hollow-spring.md)'s matriarchs responded warmly to it on
first contact.

Its biggest win yet came in Session 6: a band of Bahamut pilgrims, met
during [the Color Game](../../encounters/the-color-game.md), converted
outright — Mitch's pitch was that dragons had evolved into the Gander —
and are now moving to Briarwood as residents. Unlike the refugee converts,
these are people leaving an existing, organized faith for this one, not
just adopting a new one out of desperation.

The table describes it as a holy trinity of Goose, loosely modeled on medieval
Christianity. "The Gander" is God, the goose version, and Geezus claims the Gander is his
father. Geezus is the Holy Goose (modeled on Jesus), Mitch is the first prophet (modeled on
Paul), and Phil Bernard, the Mall King, stands roughly where the king stands in the Church
of England.

Keeping the mall clean is a focal point of the faith. In Session 7 the people of Briarwood
were not keeping it clean, and Mitch, Jack and Geezus worked together to get them to be
less dirty, very successfully.

## Goals

- Grow. Converts are, mechanically and narratively, part of how Briarwood is
  absorbing refugees right now — pledging to the Gander is bundled with
  pledging support to the mall itself.
- Whatever Mitch and Sesug's players want it to be about, beyond that — this
  file is deliberately thin on doctrine so the table can keep defining it in
  play rather than have it handed down.

## Notable Members

- Sesug Tsirch ("Geezus") — party PC, the Gander's central figure.
- Mitch Saddlerash — party PC, does the actual organizing and persuading.
- Phil Bernard — party PC and Mall King, the faith's crowned head.

## What the Party Knows

Everything — this is their own creation. As of Session 6, that includes a
new congregation of Bahamut-turned-Gander converts moving in from the
Color Game.

## DM Only

**Played for real stakes, not just a joke.** It started as table humor
(the name, "Geezus," the miracle beats — feeding a refugee crowd with one
cinnamon roll and a slice of jerky) but has real mechanical and narrative
weight now: it's actively converting refugees, it's tied to Briarwood's
admission economy, and the Hollow Spring liked it. Keep playing it with a
straight face rather than undercutting it — the bit works because the table
is taking the consequences seriously even while the premise stays funny.

**Where this could go.** A homegrown religion with real converts is a strong
long-term lever — leverage against the [Coster Council](coster-council.md),
a target for something that wants to co-opt or destroy it, or eventually a
complication for the party's own authority if it grows faster than they can
steer it. No commitment needed yet; just don't let it quietly disappear.

**The Bahamut converts (Session 6).** Formerly Shrine Row pilgrims — played
at the table first as "the Bahamut hippies," then more precisely as Bahamut
devotees — found at the Wells Fargo Regional Office having failed to find a
Color Game token. Mitch's conversion pitch ("dragons evolved into the
Gander") worked outright rather than needing a slow build, which is a
notably faster and more total win than the Hollow Spring's warmer-but-still-
cautious reception. Worth deciding whether they bring real Bahamut lore or
grievances with them — a lapsed platinum-dragon cult arriving with genuine
institutional memory is a richer addition than a blank-slate refugee band.
