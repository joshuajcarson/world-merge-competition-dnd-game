# The Circuit

## Description

Bellcross, Texas's drug trade, run out of two storefronts at
[Briarwood Mall](../places/briarwood-mall.md) that don't look like they belong to
the same operation: [Radio Shack](../places/briarwood-mall.md) for comms and
countersurveillance, [Orange Julius](../places/briarwood-mall.md) for cash and
product. It works because it's boring — a mall kiosk clerk and a smoothie counter,
easy to walk past a hundred times without a second look. It's run, quietly, by a
DEA agent who found out that being the one paid to stop the drug trade is the best
possible cover for running it.

## Goals

- Pre-merge: keep the operation invisible, keep the cash and product moving through
  Radio Shack and Orange Julius, and keep [Wade Kessler](../npcs/wade-kessler.md)'s
  badge clean enough to keep doing both jobs.
- Post-merge, once the shock wears off: convert what's left of the crew's local
  standing, hardware, and Kessler's instinct for command into control of Briarwood
  Mall itself — its supplies, its anchors, and whatever's left of its four hundred
  civilians — before anyone else organizes first.
- Frame the takeover as protection and order, not conquest, for as long as that
  story holds.

## Notable Members

- [Wade Kessler](../npcs/wade-kessler.md) — DEA Special Agent and the operation's

- [Sonny Trujillo](../npcs/sonny-trujillo.md) — runs the Radio Shack side: comms,

- [Patrice Odum](../npcs/patrice-odum.md) — runs the Orange Julius side: cash and

## DM Only

**Structure, not a gang.** This should never play like street thugs. Kessler runs
it like the case file it's disguised as — compartmentalized, quiet, allergic to
attention. That discipline is exactly what makes them dangerous once the mall
becomes the whole world: they're the only local group that already knows how to
organize covertly and enforce compliance without it looking like force.

**Positioning.** Built to be the campaign's Act 1 primary antagonist — see
[Wade Kessler](../npcs/wade-kessler.md)'s DM Only section for the full arc,
including the branch where an existential outside threat (the
[Coster Council](coster-council.md)'s hired swords, [Cult of Tiamat](cult-of-tiamat.md)
cells, or worse) flips Kessler into a reluctant, self-interested, and entirely
temporary ally against something that would raze the mall rather than merely take
it. The faction's own goals don't change in that branch — only who's currently more
dangerous to them than the party is.

**Scale.** Kessler, Trujillo, and Odum are the named crew at the mall the night of
the merge; assume a handful more unnamed hands (stockroom, lookouts, a driver who
never made it inside) as needed for numbers. This is a local operation, not an
army — its leverage is organization and Kessler's badge, not headcount, and it
should lose badly in a straight fight against anything Scornubel sends. Its
danger to the party is political and logistical before it's ever martial.

**Open question:** whether the crew's stash — wherever it was kept the night of the
merge — comes through intact. A surviving cache of pre-merge pharmaceuticals is a
significant resource on a magicless Earth-side economy and worth deciding
deliberately rather than by default.
