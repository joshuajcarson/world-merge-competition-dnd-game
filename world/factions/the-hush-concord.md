# The Hush Concord

## Description

A gnome polity from an alternate Faerûn where the Elemental Prince of Evil Air,
Yan-C-Bin, conquered the Realms instead of Tiamat's cult. The gnomes serve him,
sincerely, as stewards of the world's one remaining wind: they pay him a held
breath a day, and he lets them use his air. See
[Hushbound Gnomes](../races/hushbound-gnomes.md) for the people and the
mechanics.

They are veterans of an earlier cohort and fan favorites, so when
[Agent](../npcs/agent.md) reopened the roster for Act Two of
[the Blood Bowl](../lore/the-blood-bowl.md) they were the first call it made.
They arrive with a camp, a tinker, a priest, a crowd-pleasing catchphrase and a
machine called the **Gale Organ**. They also arrive with a grudge that Agent
assigned them.

## Goals

- Win the Blood Bowl's first phase by taking **Briarwood Mall's Pylon**, which
  Agent has flagged as exposed. That gets the Concord a home territory in the
  most visible place on the map and removes a rival at the same stroke.
- Stay beloved. A Hush Concord victory has to be a good show, with clean terms,
  a bow to the camera, and no needless bodies.
- Bring the Prince's "gift" to a new world. [Obby](../npcs/obby-windlestraw.md)
  believes the merged world is stuffy and in need of the Calm.

## What the Party Knows

Nothing before Session 8. After the demerit lands they know the name Agent gives
them on air, the camp at the end of the lot, and whatever they see of the Organ.

## DM Only

**This is Briarwood's demerit.** The [Meridian Peak](meridian-peak.md) "demerit"
from [Session 6](../../sessions/06-the-fixed-game.md) is decided here: Agent
makes Briarwood **Phase One's designated underdog**. The mall's Pylon is flagged
"exposed", and the first new faction to arrive lands on its doorstep with a
head start and an hour of broadcast lead-in. The Concord did not ask for the
matchup, but they are professionals and they intend to win it.

**Playing them.** Not villains in their own minds. Obby offers terms, takes a
bow, and apologises for the noise in advance. The scary part is that they mean it
when they say they will take the Pylon if they must walk through the whole mall.
They cut their losses the moment the numbers go bad, and they have rehearsed
being gracious losers, because a gracious loser is invited back.

**What they know about Faerûn.** They know the Tiamat-conquered Faerûn that the
mall merged beside is "the wrong one" and are quietly delighted by it. If a
player mentions Scornubel, Obby will ask which dragon won. Hearing the answer
will visibly please him. They have not met the Cult of Tiamat yet.

**Future hooks.** Defeated and still liked by the audience, the Concord can
become a **vassal or ally** (they make good Pylon-wardens and have excellent
engineers), or an annoying recurring rival that Agent keeps rebooking.
[Mitch](../people/mitch-saddlerash.md)'s experience as a performer is the
sharpest tool the party has against a faction that lives on its screen time.
