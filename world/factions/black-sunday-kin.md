# The Black Sunday Kin

## Description

Survivors of an earlier cohort's Earth fragment: a Cimarron County, Oklahoma
farming community pulled out of **April 14, 1935** (Black Sunday) at the worst of
the Dust Bowl. The merge and a long run on the broadcast changed them into
antlered, long-eared jackalope-folk: still farm families in feed-sack dresses and
overalls, still polite to a fault, and now quick enough to outrun a dust storm.
They are crowd favorites for their hospitality and for how badly the cameras
love a bonnet in a dust cloud.

They hold [Cimarron Flats](../places/cimarron-flats.md) and its Pylon. They do
not want a war. They want the cameras to see them *fair*.

## Goals

- Keep the Flats, and in Phase One reach enough territories to stay in.
- Settle disputes the Kin way: a **derby** (a contest of speed, nerve and
  neighbourliness), before anyone draws a rifle.

memory of what happened to the last people who "borrowed" their water.
