# The Ashvein Company

## Description

A duergar raiding and slaving band working a stretch of Underdark tunnel near
where a Fulgurite population came through the merge. Practical, unsentimental,
and quick to recognize a captured Fulgurite as a weapon rather than a person —
see [Grinder](../npcs/grinder.md), the shock-trooper they've built their raids
around.

## Goals

- Keep their captured Fulgurite combat-effective and controlled.
- Expand their raiding territory at the expense of anyone weaker — Fulgurite
  bands, other Underdark scavengers, whoever's in the way.

## Notable Members

- [Grinder](../npcs/grinder.md) — their Fulgurite shock-trooper.

## What the Party Knows

Nothing yet. This company operates entirely on the Underdark side of the world,
with no established connection to the party's own side of the merge line —
until [Agent](../npcs/agent.md) decides otherwise. See Grinder's file.
