# The Bar Bar Clan

## Description

Undefeated winners of an earlier competition, announced by [Agent](../npcs/agent.md) in
[Session 8](../../sessions/08-pylons-and-moonshine.md) as joining the party's game. The gnomes and
sloth that raided Briarwood's [Pylon](../lore/pylons.md) right afterward were the
[Hush Concord](the-hush-concord.md) and [Deacon](../npcs/deacon.md), not the Bar Bar clan. See
[Crowd Favorites](../lore/crowd-favorites.md).

The clan is bugbears, and every one of them is named **Bar-Bar**. They took the name from the
original Bar-Bar, a legend of an earlier World Merge who had impossible reach, couldn't be killed,
and hit like a landslide. The clan has kept all three traits. Their arms are long even for
bugbears, they get back up when they should stay down, and their weapons are absurdly long
polearms. They chant their own name when they fight: ***"BAR! BAR! BAR!"***

They have volunteered to be brought back for **every Blood Bowl** since they won. No other crowd
favorite does that. The audience adores them for it.

## Goals

- Kill everything in whatever arena Agent drops them into. They hunt every other group, not just
  the contestants.
- Stay undefeated.
- Give the crowd a show. They are veterans, and they play to the cameras.

## Notable Members

- **Big Bar-Bar**, the war-chief. Large even for the clan, 20 feet of reach with the original
  Bar-Bar's polearm, and he has never been eliminated. Statblock in
  [The Underspill](../../encounters/the-underspill.md)'s Combat Notes.
- **Bar-Bar Brutes**, the rank and file. Statblock in the same place.

## What the Party Knows

Only that Agent announced them as undefeated, until
[The Underspill](../../encounters/the-underspill.md), where they hear the drums first.

## DM Only

**First appearance:** [The Underspill](../../encounters/the-underspill.md). Agent drops them into
the Underdark to hunt the gemified, the duergar, and the party.

**A way to settle it without a war.** This is the design rule in
[Crowd Favorites](../lore/crowd-favorites.md). The Bar-Bar can't be talked out of a fight, but they
can always be challenged into a better one: **single combat**, one champion against Big Bar-Bar.
If the challenger survives three rounds or takes him below half, the clan lets them pass, and
remembers them fondly as prey worth hunting again.

**What happens after The Underspill.** That depends on the ending. They might stay below, become a
recurring hunter on the surface, or, if Big Bar-Bar dies, crown a new war-chief and start chanting
the party's names. Decide based on how the session actually plays out.

**The Aether Hunger.** Like every crowd favorite, they survived a cohort that turned on itself
(see [The Aether Hunger](../lore/the-aether-hunger.md)). Their constant volunteering might be about
more than glory. Nobody has asked. That's a slow thread worth leaving alone until it matters.
