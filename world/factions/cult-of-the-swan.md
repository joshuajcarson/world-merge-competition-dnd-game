# The Cult of the Swan

## Description

An orc tribe that stopped hearing Gruumsh some years back and never told
him so much as goodbye — they simply stopped, one raiding season, and
started collecting feathers instead. What they settled on, without much
ceremony, is a devotion to beauty: specifically, plumage, and most
specifically, swans, which they have decided are the most beautiful thing
in the world and which they kill in numbers to prove it, wearing the
result. There is a real problem with this plan: they cannot reliably tell
a swan from a goose from a duck, and have never once let that slow them
down.

## Goals

- Kill the most beautiful bird anyone in the tribe has ever heard of, and
  wear it. Right now, that's the golden-egg goose loose somewhere on
  [the Preening Grounds](../places/the-preening-grounds.md) — they don't
  know it's a transformed Earth woman, only that it's supposed to be
  spectacular.
- Survive the quake tearing the fragment apart, ideally with the bird
  already claimed.

## Notable Members

  argument, the most feathers.

## DM Only

**Play the aesthetic completely straight.** This is funny on paper — an
orc tribe that can't identify waterfowl — and it stays funny exactly as
long as the tribe itself never plays it for laughs. They are utterly
sincere about beauty as a value worth killing for. The comedy is the gap
between how seriously they take it and how wrong they keep getting the
actual birds, not a wink from the NPCs themselves.

**Not automatically a fight.** The brief for this faction is explicitly
"social or violence" — they want the same thing the party might be
willing to let them have (feathers, prestige, a trophy) more than they
want a fight over principle. A party that can offer them a genuinely
impressive bird, feathers, or a face-saving story has a real chance of
talking their way past or even recruiting temporary help against the
Leprechauns, who are raiding tribal territory too and read to the tribe as
an ugly, undignified nuisance.

**If it goes to violence,** they fight for the same reason they do
everything — pride in beauty, not tactics — and a party that makes them
look ugly (mockery, humiliation, exposing a member's fake feathers) can
break their morale faster than damage will.

**Naming note.** "Cult of the Swan" is the working name from the table's
own mad-lib; nothing says the tribe calls itself that. A DM who wants an
in-fiction name for them can invent one when it matters; don't force it
before then.
