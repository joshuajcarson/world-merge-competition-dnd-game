# The Stewies

## Description

A mercenary company operating out of [Scornubel](../places/scornubel.md)'s
mercenary district, recently paid in a windfall of surplus weapons and
armor — the same stock the city expected to need against a Tiamat attack
that never came. Their infiltrators hit [Briarwood Mall](../places/briarwood-mall.md)
at night, ninja-style; every one of them carried the same tattoo, a
stylized figure the party has taken to calling "Stevie Griffin."

## Goals

- Whatever the Council member who hired them wants, which as of the raid
  looked like probing the mall's defenses and taking supplies.

## Notable Members

*(None named — the party met the company's guard captain at their district
camp, who was intimidated into standing down rather than named as a leader.
The company itself scattered when confronted.)*

## What the Party Knows

Confirmed via that guard: the Stewies were hired by a member of Scornubel's
governing Council to hit the mall. Which member, and why, is unknown — the
Council has seven members, publicly known individually but famously opaque
about how it actually makes decisions.

## DM Only

**Confirms the Coster Council's established DM notes exactly.** That file
already predicted the Council would be the first organized Faerun power to
treat Briarwood as an unguarded warehouse — the Stewies contract is that
prediction playing out. Good validation to build on rather than a surprise
to explain away.

**Open thread, worth real weight:** which of the seven Council members hired
them, and whether it was authorized by the full Council or one member
freelancing. This is a strong mid-campaign mystery — don't resolve it
casually. See [Coster Council](coster-council.md) for the parent
organization.
