# Cult of Tiamat

## Description

The dragon cult that won. Having taken the Sword Coast alongside Tiamat's surviving
children, the Cult now administers the conquered coastal cities and is pushing inland
up the River Chionthar. Chromatic-draconic heraldry is the ruling aesthetic of the
coast now, not a heresy hiding in cellars.

## What the Party Knows

Nothing yet. The party are Earth civilians from
[Briarwood Mall](../places/briarwood-mall.md) and have never heard of Tiamat.

## DM Only

Pre-merge, they took [Baldur's Gate](../places/baldurs-gate.md), left its walls
standing on purpose, and were moving upriver on
[Scornubel](../places/scornubel.md). Post-merge, the main body and the conquered coast
are elsewhere on the merged world — or nowhere; that is the DM's call. What certainly
arrives near Briarwood are the cells that had already infiltrated Scornubel's refugee
tent city. They do not yet know what the merge did to their god.

Field-issue gear is standardized enough to loot predictably — see
[Cultist's Chromatic Ampoule](../../items/cultists-chromatic-ampoule.md) for a
plausible drop off any patrol or cache.
