# The Dartwood Court

## Description

A court of tiny, brilliantly colored poison-frog people (think grung) from a
fantasy rainforest in an earlier cohort, who held the broadcast's attention for
nine weeks with their pageantry: processions on lily-pad barges, a whole
language of croaks, and a Queen who judges everything as theatre. They are crowd
favorites because they are terrifying and adorable at once.

They hold [the Verdigris Clearing](../places/verdigris-clearing.md) and its
Pylon, and will let the party pass for a **performance** good enough to move the
Queen. Failing that, they have blowguns and the high ground.

## Goals

- Keep the Clearing and be seen to keep it with *style*.
- Collect spectacle. A court that is bored is a court that attacks.

## Notable Members

- [Queen Ximena Goldleg](../npcs/queen-ximena-goldleg.md) — the Court's ruler and
  its only critic.

## DM Only

*A seed; swap them for anyone you like.* Their poison is a debuff, not death,
and every dart is theatrically labelled. They expect to lose to someone
charming and have never yet met anyone who managed it.
