# The Underspill

## Description

A stretch of Underdark several hundred feet below the
[Spillway Valley](the-spillway-valley.md). The Blue Wave broke through into it, and the flood is
still pouring down through a crack into a glowing blue sump. Faerzress makes the rock overhead
shimmer violet. The tunnels lead up through a gas-choked duergar mine, a fungus forest, a
space-folding maze, and a cracked bridge over a chasm, and all of them come out at a fortified
duergar gem-mine with two ways back to daylight.

## Notable Locations

- **The Blue Sump.** Where the party wakes. The wave's water is still falling in from above.
- **Seam 4.** An abandoned duergar mine adit, full of flammable red chokedamp.
- **The Gemlit Crossroads.** A nest of the gemified, lit red by gem clusters growing from the walls.
- **The Unmade Ways.** Faerzress-soaked tunnels that don't hold their shape.
- **The Mycelium Hall.** A vast fungus forest with shriekers and a strong updraft.
- **The Groaning Span.** The old duergar road's bridge over a chasm, cracking with aftershocks.
- **The Gem Works.** The duergar hold where captives are force-fed red gems and turned into
  gemified. It has an ore lift and a natural chimney up to the surface.

## What the Party Knows

Nothing yet. They arrive unconscious, at the start of
[The Underspill](../../encounters/the-underspill.md).

## DM Only

**Agent is here, lagged.** The rock delays and garbles Agent's feed by several seconds, but it
never cuts out. This is **not** an Agent-free zone, however much the party hopes it is.

**Locals and invaders.** The gemified and the duergar who make them are the locals here, and they
hate each other. The [Bar-Bar Clan](../factions/bar-bar-clan.md) was dropped in by Agent to hunt
both of them, and the party.

**Same gems as Fearus.** The red gems match [Fearus Dungeon](fearus-dungeon.md) exactly, and so
does the force-feeding operation. Whether these duergar are the
[Ashvein Company](../factions/ashvein-company.md), the Fearus operation, or something else is still
Fearus Dungeon's open question. Not settled here.

**Fulgurites.** The [Fulgurites](../races/fulgurites.md)' Earth fragment landed somewhere in the
Underdark too. Nothing ties it to this stretch yet. A lone Fulgurite is in the encounter's
Complication Bank as a hint.
