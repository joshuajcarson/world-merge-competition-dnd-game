# The Drowned Chantry

## Description

Two bodies of water that had nothing to do with each other now occupy the
same hole in the ground, and the ruin sitting in it doesn't fully belong to
either side. Half of it is a modest Faerun shrine to **Eldath** — pale stone
columns, a cracked reflecting pool, a chantry porch where swanmays once kept
watch over the Reaching Stream — and half of it is an ordinary Bellcross
municipal park: a gazebo, a boathouse, two rusting paddle boats, and a
feed-bin sign that still reads **PLEASE DO NOT FEED THE SWANS** in cheerful
parks-department stencil. The merge dropped both into the same footprint at
once, and the water rose to cover the seams — stone steps disappear into
duckweed, a paddle boat is wedged sideways between two chantry columns,
prayer-reeds grow up through a chain-link fence that goes nowhere.

Nothing about it announces itself from a distance. It reads as a pond with
some ruins in it, easy to walk past if the birds aren't visible yet. They
usually are, eventually — pale shapes standing very still at the tree line,
watching whoever's come to the water's edge, in no hurry to be the first to
move.

## Notable Locations

- **The Boathouse Nave** — the entrance from dry ground: half dock, half
  chantry porch, roofed on the Earth side and open-columned on the Faerun
  side where the two structures overlap. The first place a party meets any
  [Swanfolk](../races/swanfolk.md), usually sentries rather than the whole
  flock.
- **The Gazebo Roost** — a collapsed park gazebo, half-sunk, used as a
  vantage and nesting perch. Good sightlines over the whole pool; whoever
  holds it sees everyone else coming.
- **The Reflecting Pool** — the deep water at the center, where the original
  chantry's sacred pool and the park lake's deepest point landed on top of
  each other. [Sister Maerwyn](../npcs/sister-maerwyn.md) never leaves it.
  This is the dungeon's real heart, not its literal middle room.
- **The Nesting Undercroft** — the flooded storage level beneath the
  chantry: cold-cellar on one side, park maintenance shed on the other,
  ankle-to-waist deep throughout. Nests, eggs, scavenged Earth and Faerun
  odds and ends dragged down by the flock, and whatever loot the DM wants to
  seed as the dungeon's actual reward.
- **The Feed Path** — the muddy shoreline trail ringing the pool, the one
  route that stays mostly above water. The obvious route in, which makes it
  the obvious place for the flock to watch.

## What the Party Knows

Nothing. Neither [Briarwood](briarwood-mall.md) nor
[Scornubel](scornubel.md) has sent anyone this far into the ground between
them yet.

## DM Only

- **Function.** This is one answer — not the whole answer — to the open
  question both [Scornubel](scornubel.md) and
  [Briarwood Mall](briarwood-mall.md) leave hanging in their own DM notes:
  what the merge actually left in the unclaimed ground between the two
  starting locations. Place the chantry somewhere inside whatever distance
  the DM has already set for that gap; it doesn't resolve the gap by itself,
  it's a single flooded pocket within it; the rest of that ground can be
  anything else.
- **Timeline.** Simultaneous with the main merge, not before or after —
  see [Swanfolk](../races/swanfolk.md)'s DM notes. There is no pre-merge
  snapshot to write for this location the way Scornubel and Briarwood get
  one; it has only ever existed in its current, fused state.
- **Why it's worth finding.** It's the closest source of fresh water to
  either starting location if the merge didn't bring a full river or the
  mall's own plumbing fails — which makes the Swanfolk's territorial
  instinct a genuine problem rather than a set-dressing threat the moment
  either side's water runs low. That's the practical hook; the tragic one
  is [Sister Maerwyn](../npcs/sister-maerwyn.md) and the open question about
  what's recoverable in her.
- **[Rosalind Voss](../npcs/rosalind-voss.md)** is the first-contact figure
  — see her file for how to run the Boathouse Nave as a conversation instead
  of an ambush, if the party gives her the room to be one.
- **Running it as a full crawl.** This file gives the location and its
  rooms; it doesn't stat out a node graph, triggers, or a combat-by-combat
  breakdown the way [Mall Rats](../../encounters/mall-rats.md) does for
  [Sector 7](cohort-14-holding-sector.md). Build one with the
  `encounter-weaver` skill against this file and
  [Swanfolk](../races/swanfolk.md) if the table's ready to actually run it.
- **Open question:** what's actually in the Nesting Undercroft. Left
  unseeded on purpose — decide it against whatever the party's tier and the
  campaign's item economy need when they go looking.
