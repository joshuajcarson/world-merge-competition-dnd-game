# Fearus Dungeon

## Description

A thick jungle on the other side of a portal that opened in Chess World —
found while the party was still dealing with the first wave of "gemified"
attackers back at the mall. Red gems grow out of everything here, the same
material embedded in the bodies of the humanoids attacking Briarwood. At the
jungle's heart, a duergar encampment was found forcing captive humanoids to
consume the gems.

## Notable Locations

- The clearing the party made by triggering a chain reaction of gem
  explosions — a fast, loud way through, at the cost of the local
  ecosystem, which drew a hostile response from whatever locals depend on
  it.
- The duergar encampment where captives are force-fed gems.

## What the Party Knows

Everything they found firsthand: the jungle, the gems, the locals defending
it, and the duergar operation at the center. The connection between the
gem-corrupted attackers back at the mall and this place is established;
nothing beyond that.

## DM Only

**Naming note.** "Fearus" almost certainly started as a mishearing or typo
for "Faerun" at the table and has stuck as the place's actual working name —
treated here as a proper noun rather than corrected, since that's what the
table will call it going forward.

**Open question, worth a deliberate answer: is this connected to the
Fulgurites?** The MO here — duergar forcing captive humanoids through a bodily
corruption process — closely parallels
[the Ashvein Company](../factions/ashvein-company.md)'s established operation
against [Fulgurites](../races/fulgurites.md) in the Underdark, except the
mechanism here is gem-consumption rather than faerzress exposure, and the
setting is a surface jungle rather than the Underdark proper. Two honest
options: (1) this is a *different* duergar operation running a parallel
racket, entirely unconnected to Ashvein Company — the coincidence is just
duergar being duergar; or (2) this is Ashvein Company itself, run a portal
or a relocated operation, using gems as a distinct corruption method from
Overcharge. Pick one before the party investigates further; nothing here
commits to either.

**The "gemified" attackers.** Naked, glowing-gem-studded humanoids, hostile
to anyone ungemified — first encountered smashing into Chess World before
the portal was found. No formal statblock or race write-up exists yet;
build one only if the table returns to this thread. For now, treat them as
this dungeon's equivalent of the Fulgurite Overcharge trait: an involuntary,
externally-imposed transformation turning ordinary captives into a threat,
not a race anyone chose.

**Ecosystem hook.** The locals who attacked over the destroyed clearing were
never identified beyond "locals" — a good seed for a return trip if the
party wants to make amends or the DM wants a consequence for the explosive
approach.
