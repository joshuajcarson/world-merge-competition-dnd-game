# The Preening Grounds

## Description

A Faerun wetland fragment — reed-choked marsh, slow black water, and enough
waterfowl that a tribe of orcs decided it was worth building a religion
around. The Cult of the Swan has claimed it as sacred hunting ground for
as long as anyone there remembers, which isn't very long; fragments don't
come with much history attached. Now it's a fragment with three problems
at once: a raiding Luck of Aether Leprechauns has moved in from a
neighboring shard, a golden-egg-laying goose the whole tribe wants is
hiding somewhere in the reeds, and the ground itself has started to go —
deep, irregular cracks opening in the mud, standing water draining into
them, tremors getting more frequent instead of less.

## DM Only

**The quake clock.** This is the fragment's countdown, structurally the
same device as the Aether Tide/rising-water pattern used elsewhere
(see `the-color-game.md` and `the-long-climb.md` for precedent) — don't
run it as a single die roll or a hard deadline, run it as an escalating
series of DM-narrated tremors that make the terrain worse each time:
first cracked ground and startled birds, then reed-beds collapsing into
new sinkholes and standing water draining away, finally open fissures and
whole sections of marsh dropping out from under anyone standing on them.
Tie major story beats (finding Carol, the Cult confrontation, the
Leprechaun raids) to tremor stages rather than a strict turn count, so
pacing can flex to however fast or slow the table is actually moving.

**Layout, loosely.** Reed marsh and open black water at the center where
the Cult does most of its "hunting" (see
[the Cult of the Swan](../factions/cult-of-the-swan.md)); drier hummocks
and thicket at the edges where
[Gildrot's Luck](../npcs/gildrot.md) has been raiding in from; Carol (see
[Carol](../npcs/carol.md)) is hiding somewhere in between, drawn to
neither faction and actively avoiding both. Don't over-map this — it only
needs to support "the party picks a direction and something happens,"
per [The Golden Goose Chase](../../encounters/the-golden-goose-chase.md).

**After.** If the fragment goes down before the party gets clear, that's a
legitimate outcome, not a failure state — see the encounter file's ending
options. If it survives (quake contained, slows, or the party simply
outruns it), the Cult and whatever's left of the Luck are both still here
afterward and can recur.
