# Briarwood Mall

## Description

> **BRIARWOOD MALL — WHERE BELLCROSS COMES TOGETHER!**
> Two levels. Four anchors. Over ninety stores under one roof.
> Free parking. Always.

Thursday, **November 5th, 1992**. Fifty-eight degrees and falling, which in Bellcross counts as sweater weather, and the fountain court has been running its holiday water feature since Halloween because nobody at Mall Management wants to be the one who says it's too early. The skylights are going dark by five-thirty now. Under them the whole building smells like Cinnabon, floor wax, and the ozone off the arcade.

The election was Tuesday. **Ross Perot** won it, and Briarwood has not stopped talking about it for two days — a Texan in the White House, charts and all, and every third conversation in the food court is somebody's brother-in-law explaining what happens to the deficit now. The Radio Shack has a TV in the window tuned to CNN with the sound off. The Waldenbooks has already moved the paperback biographies to a front table.

And tonight, **7:00 PM sharp**, in the storefront that was a Lerner New York until August:

> **GRAND OPENING! "AS SEEN ON TV" — IT'S NOT ON TV ANYMORE. IT'S HERE.**
> Every product you've ordered at 2 AM, now under one roof. The Chop-O-Matic. The Miracle Blade. The Clapper. Ginsu. Ronco. **And LIVE, IN PERSON, ONE NIGHT ONLY —**
> **SUZANNE SOMERS**, star of television, demonstrating the sensational **ThighMaster®**!
> Meet her! Get an autograph! *First 200 customers receive a FREE tote!*
> **Refreshments provided. Bring the whole family. You've seen the commercial. Now live it.**

The line for it starts forming outside Chess King around four. Mall security has put up the retractable belt stanchions, which are the good ones, which means somebody upstairs thinks this is a big deal.

## Notable Locations

- **The "As Seen On TV" store, lower level, near the fountain** — site of tonight's grand opening. Folding chairs, a low riser stage, a card table stacked with ThighMasters, and a banner that has one corner sagging.
- **The fountain court** — a two-story open well with a tile fountain, benches, and pennies. Everything in the building is navigated by reference to it.
- **The food court, upper level** — Sbarro, Orange Julius, Hot Dog on a Stick, Chick-fil-A, Cinnabon. Bolted-down tables. One tray-return nobody uses.
- **Aladdin's Castle arcade** — dark, loud, sticky carpet, *Street Fighter II* cabinet with a permanent crowd.
- **Anchors** — Dillard's and JCPenney at the ends, Sears on the west wing (auto center out back, tire bay, roll-up doors), Montgomery Ward on the east.
- **The rest of the promenade** — Sam Goody, Waldenbooks, Spencer Gifts, Contempo Casuals, Merry-Go-Round, Foot Locker, KB Toys, Suncoast Motion Picture Company, Claire's, Piercing Pagoda, GNC, Radio Shack, Sunglass Hut, Thom McAn, a Payless, a Hallmark, and a photo booth outside the restrooms that eats quarters.
- **Back-of-house** — the service corridor ringing the building: loading dock, dumpster bay, the security office with its bank of black-and-white monitors, the mechanical room, and the janitor's closets, one per wing.

## What the Party Knows

It is a mall. It is Thursday. Suzanne Somers is coming at seven.

## DM Only

- **Function.** This is the Session 0 table — the last ordinary hour of the Earth world, played straight and at length. Let the players shop, eat, argue about Perot, wait in line, and be bored. The longer the mundanity runs, the harder the merge lands. Nothing in this file is meant to be threatening; the horror is entirely retroactive.
- **The merge.** Briarwood is on the Earth-side merge line described in [the Session 0 primer](../session-zero-primer.md). Whatever the party is doing at the moment it hits, the building is what survives with them — which makes the mall's inventory the campaign's opening equipment list. Sears auto center: tire irons, jacks, chain. KB Toys: nothing useful, which will become a running joke. GNC: calories. Radio Shack: batteries, and every radio in Bellcross about to receive 100,000 channels at once.
- **The neighbor.** Briarwood does not land alone. [Scornubel](scornubel.md) — a Faerun caravan city caught mid-muster, with a hired army, a merchant council, and thousands of refugees in it — and a stretch of its river valley come through close by. That is the campaign's first external pressure and it dwarfs the mall: roughly four hundred Earth civilians against an organized force an order of magnitude larger, professionally violent, and braced for a dragon attack that is no longer coming. Set the distance between the two before Session 1 — parking-lot adjacent means contact in the first session, a day's walk buys the party a few sessions to scavenge first. The Faerun side's read on what this building *is* (fortress, omen, or warehouse) is the opening move; see that file's DM notes.
- **Suzanne Somers.** [She is here in person](../npcs/suzanne-somers.md) and therefore comes through the merge. A recognizable Earth celebrity stranded in a cosmic bloodsport is a strong recurring NPC and an even stronger broadcast asset — the Administration's audience will find her *very* watchable. Decide before Session 1 whether she survives the first hour.
- **The janitor's closets** are load-bearing — and after the merge, Briarwood has one more of them than it had before. See [Mall Security Keyring](../../items/mall-security-keyring.md): that guard worked at *Westfield* Mall, a different building entirely, and his closet — door shut, teenager inside — comes through the merge and lands in a Briarwood service corridor where no closet has ever been. The party can walk past a door they've walked past all evening and find it isn't the same door. Reminder when you run the back-of-house: count the closets, one per wing, so the extra one reads as wrong the moment it appears.
- **Crowd size.** Call it four hundred people in the building at 7 PM, plus staff. That number is the campaign's Earth-refugee population if the merge takes the whole structure — decide now, because it determines whether the party are survivors among many or the only ones. It is also the number that gets weighed against Scornubel's muster the first time anyone on either side counts.
- **The mall's real defenses are doors and lights.** Worth knowing before first contact, because the party will reach for them: the anchors' roll-up security grilles, the Sears auto center's bay doors, the loading dock, and a building with no ground-floor windows. None of it was built to stop anything, but it *reads* as a fortification to someone who has spent a month building a palisade — which is exactly the misunderstanding that gets the mall stormed. Power and lighting are the other half; a lit, humming slab at night is either a beacon or a target and the party gets to choose which.
- **Open question:** does the merge take the parking lot? Cars, gas, and the highway change what the first three sessions look like — and now it is also the ground between Briarwood and [Scornubel](scornubel.md). Whatever the merge leaves out there is the field the first meeting happens on.
