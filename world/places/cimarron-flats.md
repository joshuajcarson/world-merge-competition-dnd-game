# Cimarron Flats

## Description

Dust, to the horizon. Dead wheat, a dry creek bed, sage and tumbleweed piled against
leaning fence posts, drifts of fine red-brown dust up to the bottom wire of every
barbed-wire fence, and a skeleton of a windmill with no wheel left on it. Anything
that was ever a field has been scoured to hardpan. The sky is the color of weak
tea, and a rolling black wall of dust sits on the northern edge, patient and
very tall. It is April 14, 1935, forever.

## Notable Locations

- **The windmill** — the Pylon stands at its foot, Agent-branded, humming faintly
  in a landscape with nothing else making a sound.
- **The fence line** — a collapsed barbed-wire fence running across the plain;
  the Kin's last defensive line.
- **The dry creek** — a cut bank a few feet deep, the best cover on the map.
- **The Quill farm** — a leaning shell of a house and a barn the Kin live out of.
