# The Verdigris Clearing

## Description

A small, almost perfectly round clearing in a rainforest so thick that the
sky is a rumor. The ground is black mud and moss; a shallow pool fed by a
trickle of water lies in the middle, with a stone altar half drowned in it; and a
giant fallen ceiba tree, long dead and furred with ferns, bromeliads and
orchids, lies across one side. The canopy is a hundred feet up and every branch
of it moves. The air smells of wet leaves and something sweet.

## Notable Locations

- **The altar pool** — the Pylon rises from a weathered stone altar in the pool.
- **The ceiba log** — fallen trunk, cover and a walkway above the mud.
- **The buttress roots** — a ring of waist-high root walls on the eastern edge.
- **The Court's pavilion** — a palm-leaf throne under the ceiba's crown,
  with the lily-pad barges moored beside it.
