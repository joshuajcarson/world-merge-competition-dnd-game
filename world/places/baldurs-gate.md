# Baldur's Gate

## Description

The great port at the mouth of the Chionthar, and the largest city to fall in the
dragon conquest of the Sword Coast. It was taken, not razed — the walls are intact,
which the refugees who reached [Scornubel](scornubel.md) find more frightening than
if they had been thrown down.

## Notable Locations

## What the Party Knows

It has fallen. It is held by the [Cult of Tiamat](../factions/cult-of-tiamat.md).
No reliable word has come upriver since Highharvestide.

## DM Only

Deliberately thin — the point is that nobody knows what is happening inside. Fill in
only when the party has reason to go, or when a refugee's account needs to be
contradicted later.
