# Sector 7 (Cohort 15 Holding)

## Description

Storage space, not a world — cramped concrete corridors and prop rooms behind
whatever [Agent](../npcs/agent.md) actually broadcasts, full of leftover set dressing and
"ambient hazard content" from a cohort eight seasons before this one. It touches
[Briarwood Mall](../places/briarwood-mall.md) through a merge-displaced closet that
shouldn't be there. See [Mall Rats](../../encounters/mall-rats.md) for the full
layout and the scene set here.

## Notable Locations

- Nooks and junctions detailed room-by-room in [Mall Rats](../../encounters/mall-rats.md)'s node graph.

## What the Party Knows

Cleared in [Session 1](../../sessions/01-mall-rats-and-the-grasslands-city.md).
The sector's surviving ratkin — including [Ironquill](../npcs/ironquill.md)
and nineteen others — now live at Briarwood Mall rather than here.

## DM Only

Was home to the last of a ratkin population the Administration cancelled mid-cohort and
never bothered to dispose of — see [Mall Rats](../../encounters/mall-rats.md) for
their situation and [Agent](../npcs/agent.md)'s mocking tone toward them. **Renamed
from "Cohort 14" to "Cohort 15"** per Ironquill's own statement in play (see
Session 1's DM notes) — update any other reference found later that still says 14.

As of Session 1 the sector itself is cleared and its garrison relocated; whether
Sector 7 as a *place* still exists in some form (empty, resealed, repurposed by
the Administration) or is simply gone is an open question, same posture as
before — decide only if the party goes looking for it again.

The same bleed-through happens pre-merge on the Faerun side: see
[Deputy Registrar's Log — Scornubel](../lore/deputy-registrars-log-scornubel.md) for
unexplained new doors and a sealed building appearing in Scornubel days before the
merge itself. Whether that Faerun-side anomaly connects to this same sector is
undecided — pick an answer only if the party pursues it from both ends.
