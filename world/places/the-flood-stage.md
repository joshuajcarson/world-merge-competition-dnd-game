# The Flood Stage

## Description

A purpose-built arena, not a real district — [Agent](../npcs/agent.md)
assembled it, whole, for [the Color Game](../../encounters/the-color-game.md),
and pulled contestants directly out of [Briarwood Mall](briarwood-mall.md)
to fill it, the same "time freezes" teleportation Agent's used since
[Session 0](../../sessions/00-welcome-to-the-world-merge-competition.md).
It reads as a small commercial district — a bank, a toy store, a medical
supply warehouse, a multi-story dance studio, an apartment building lit up
in neon — with just enough believable clutter and signage to pass as a real
place at a glance, while being flooded on cue as part of the game's own
design.

## Notable Locations

The DM's own map sorts every building into four height tiers, matching the
Color Game's flood clock exactly — see **The Flood Order**, below.

**Tall (never floods before the event ends):**
- **27 Dance Studios Stacked On Top of Each Other** — exactly what it
  sounds like; the arena's tallest structure by a wide margin.
- **Wells Fargo Regional Office** — where the party found the Color
  Game's Microwave token. One of two confirmed safe fallback points.
- **Neon Lights Apartment** — where the party met
  [Miriel Ashgrove](../npcs/miriel-ashgrove.md), crowned Scornubel's
  "Queen" for the event. The other confirmed safe fallback point.

**Medium (floods third, after Short):**
- **Multi-story Dance Studio** — the party's fallback point once the
  Short-tier buildings started going under.
- **Medical Supplies Factory** — where an unidentified sniper fired on
  [Phil Bernard](../people/phil-bernard.md) during
  [Session 3](../../sessions/03-the-flooded-crown.md). See DM Only.
- **Younkers** — isolated from the rest of the arena's cluster; not yet
  visited.

**Short (floods second):**
- **Toy Store** — called "the Toys R Us" at the table during Session 3;
  same location. Site of the corrupted puma fight; a group of Bahamut
  pilgrims ended up stranded on its roof as the flood rose.
- **Strip Mall** — unvisited as of Session 3.
- **Industrial Chicken Coop** — unvisited as of Session 3.

**Shortest (floods first):**
- **Fallen Tower** — a collapsed structure, short by damage rather than
  design; presumably more Administration backstage debris than a real
  building.
- **Caravan of Junkyard Vans** — mobile rather than built, parked between
  the Multi-story Dance Studio and the Strip Mall/Neon Lights Apartment
  cluster.
- **McDonald's** — unvisited as of Session 3.

**The water itself, not a building:** at least two enormous, serpentine
Tide-Titans prowl the flooded gaps between the clusters — one in the
western channel (between 27 Dance Studios/Strip Mall and the rest), one in
the central channel (between the Multi-story Dance Studio/Fallen Tower
cluster and the McDonald's/Medical Supplies Factory cluster). See
[the Color Game](../../encounters/the-color-game.md)'s Tide-Titans section
— avoid or redirect, never fight.

## The Flood Order

Confirmed, not estimated: height tier determines flood order exactly.
**Shortest floods first, then Short, then Medium. Tall buildings do not
flood before the event ends** — Wells Fargo Regional Office and Neon
Lights Apartment are the arena's two guaranteed safe points, full stop.
Use this in place of any generic depth-tier guess when running the Aether
Tide — see that mechanic's entry in
[the Color Game](../../encounters/the-color-game.md) for how it interacts
with the rest of the event.

## What the Party Knows

The site of the Color Game — assembled overnight, flooded on a rising
clock as the game itself demanded, and reached only because Agent pulled
them there straight out of Briarwood. Whether it persists after the game
ends or gets struck like a set is something the party hasn't had reason to
find out yet.

## DM Only

**Where the set dressing comes from.** Almost certainly the same
Administration backstage material [Sector 7](cohort-15-holding-sector.md)
draws on — "leftover set dressing" from a prior cohort, dressed up as a
plausible small-town commercial strip rather than left as raw storage
space. Agent has clearly gotten more confident staging elaborate one-off
sets since Session 0's straightforward "time freezes and reality changes"
approach.

**Reusable.** Nothing says this arena only exists once — a DM who wants a
recurring "the broadcast built a stage for this" venue for future Blood
Bowl-adjacent spectacle events has a ready-made one here rather than
needing to invent a new backstage space each time. Decide whether the Aug
27 arena specifically gets struck and rebuilt, or genuinely persists
somewhere, only if the party goes looking for it again.

**Meridian Peak, current position (spoiler — never reveal directly).**
Holding the Medical Supplies Factory, guarding the Gamma token — see
[the Color Game](../../encounters/the-color-game.md)'s Node D, which is
this building specifically. The Factory is Medium tier, meaning it floods
after Short but before the event ends: Meridian Peak will have to abandon
it and relocate to a Tall building — **Wells Fargo Regional Office or
Neon Lights Apartment** — before it goes under, or drown holding Gamma
uncontested. This is a real, plannable evacuation, not flavor: a party
that reads the map can beat them to either safe point, cut off their
retreat, or simply wait for them to be forced into the open. Absent party
interference, assume they make the call late rather than early — they'd
rather hold Gamma as long as possible.
