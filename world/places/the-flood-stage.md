# The Flood Stage

## Description

A purpose-built arena, not a real district — [Agent](../npcs/agent.md)
assembled it, whole, for [the Color Game](../../encounters/the-color-game.md),
and pulled contestants directly out of [Briarwood Mall](briarwood-mall.md)
to fill it, the same "time freezes" teleportation Agent's used since
[Session 0](../../sessions/00-welcome-to-the-world-merge-competition.md).
It reads as a small commercial district — a bank, a toy store, a medical
supply warehouse, a multi-story dance studio, an apartment building lit up
in neon — with just enough believable clutter and signage to pass as a real
place at a glance, while being flooded on cue as part of the game's own
design.

## Notable Locations

- **The Wells Fargo** — where the party found the Color Game's Microwave
  token.
- **The Toys "R" Us** — site of the corrupted puma fight; a group of
  Bahamut pilgrims ended up stranded on its roof as the flood rose.
- **The medical supply factory** — where an unidentified sniper fired on
  [Phil Bernard](../people/phil-bernard.md) during
  [Session 3](../../sessions/03-the-flooded-crown.md).
- **The multi-story dance studio** — the party's fallback point once the
  lower buildings started going under.
- **The neon-lit apartment building** — where the party met
  [Miriel Ashgrove](../npcs/miriel-ashgrove.md), crowned Scornubel's
  "Queen" for the event.

## What the Party Knows

The site of the Color Game — assembled overnight, flooded on a rising
clock as the game itself demanded, and reached only because Agent pulled
them there straight out of Briarwood. Whether it persists after the game
ends or gets struck like a set is something the party hasn't had reason to
find out yet.

## DM Only

**Where the set dressing comes from.** Almost certainly the same
Administration backstage material [Sector 7](cohort-15-holding-sector.md)
draws on — "leftover set dressing" from a prior cohort, dressed up as a
plausible small-town commercial strip rather than left as raw storage
space. Agent has clearly gotten more confident staging elaborate one-off
sets since Session 0's straightforward "time freezes and reality changes"
approach.

**Reusable.** Nothing says this arena only exists once — a DM who wants a
recurring "the broadcast built a stage for this" venue for future Blood
Bowl-adjacent spectacle events has a ready-made one here rather than
needing to invent a new backstage space each time. Decide whether the Aug
27 arena specifically gets struck and rebuilt, or genuinely persists
somewhere, only if the party goes looking for it again.
