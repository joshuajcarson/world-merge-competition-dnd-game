# The Spillway Valley

## Description

An open grassland valley, Earth-or-Faerun in texture rather than Aether — ordinary grass, ordinary
wind, nothing oversized or violet-shimmering grazing in it. A wide river runs the length of the
valley floor. Follow it north and the valley narrows toward a dam, visible only as a pale line
against the horizon from most of the valley — big enough to be the river's actual source, not a
local mill dam.

## Notable Locations

- **The river** — the valley's main obstacle; wide, not especially deep, but enough current to
  matter. See [The Blue Wave](../../encounters/the-blue-wave.md) for the crossing.
- **The dam** — far enough north that only a good look (DC 20 Perception) resolves any detail on
  it at all. What little can be made out looks like a fight in progress. Who's fighting, and why,
  is not established — see that encounter's DM Only.

## What the Party Knows

Nothing yet — first visit is [The Blue Wave](../../encounters/the-blue-wave.md), heading west from
the shed in search of another Agent-free zone.

## DM Only

**Not a Blood Bowl Holding.** Unlike Cimarron Flats, the Verdigris Clearing, or Featherline Flats,
this valley isn't framed as a Pylon hex — it's part of the "west" thread from
[Session 9](../../sessions/09-a-glitch-in-the-signal.md)'s shed graffiti, a separate arc from the
territory battle. `controlled_by` is `null` on purpose.

**Agent is watching here.** Unlike the shed, this valley is ordinary broadcast territory — Agent
re-established contact with the party at the valley's edge (see
[The Blue Wave](../../encounters/the-blue-wave.md), Node A) and is narrating normally for
everything that happens here.

**The dam is a loaded Chekov's gun, not yet fired.** It explodes by the end of
[The Blue Wave](../../encounters/the-blue-wave.md), and whatever battle the party glimpsed from a
distance is implied to be why. Who was actually fighting up there is deliberately undecided —
don't invent it until the table needs the answer.
