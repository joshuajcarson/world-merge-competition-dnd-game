# Jack's Spectrometer

*Wondrous Item, Uncommon*

This device requires no attunement and has no charges — it simply works,
as many times a day as you can spare the action for it.

**Sweep the Spectrum.** As an action, point the spectrometer at your
surroundings. Anything within 30 feet emitting radiation outside the
visible spectrum — radio, microwave, infrared, ultraviolet, x-ray, or gamma
— registers automatically. You learn that something's there and roughly
which band it's on. No check required.

The spectrometer is not magical. It does not detect as magic, is unaffected
by antimagic, and cannot be dispelled — it is a machine, built by a person,
that happens to do something this cosmos has no other vocabulary for.

## Flavor

A chest-strapped box of salvaged dials, a cracked oscilloscope screen, and
an antenna array that makes [Jack Mercer](../world/people/jack-mercer.md)'s
already-considerable Goliath silhouette read, at a glance, like a very large
astromech droid — the resemblance was immediate and the table has not let
him live it down since. It hums when it's working and beeps, unhelpfully,
at almost everything else.

## DM Notes

- **Why it's here:** the result of one of Jack's long-term projects — this
  campaign is running Blades in the Dark-style downtime projects, and this
  was his. Built from mall electronics-store salvage and stubbornness, not
  found loot.
- **Confirmed in play (Session 3):** this is what actually let the party
  locate [the Color Game](../encounters/the-color-game.md)'s
  non-visible-spectrum tokens without groping around blind — treat "immediate,
  automatic detection within 30 feet" as this item's real function, not
  flavor color, and expect Jack to reach for it in any future scene involving
  non-visible radiation.
- **Balance note:** detection only, no offense or defense — it tells you
  where the interesting thing is, it doesn't do anything about it once
  you're there.
