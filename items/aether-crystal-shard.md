# Aether Crystal Shard

*Wondrous Item, Uncommon*

As a Bonus Action, you crush this crystal against a weapon or spellcasting
focus you're holding. The next attack roll or spell attack you make before
the end of your turn, if it hits, deals an extra 2d6 Force damage as the
crystal's stored charge discharges into the strike. If you don't attack
before the end of your turn, the charge dissipates harmlessly and the crystal
is still spent.

## Flavor

Fist-sized, red, and warm before you even touch it — real aether crystal, the
raw material the Administration's collection rigs were built to strip-mine
this cohort's Aether world for. It hums unevenly, like it can't settle on a
pitch, and the hum gets worse for a few seconds right before someone uses it,
like it knows.

## DM Notes

- **Why it's here:** Aether-world salvage — a downed piece of Administration
  collection equipment, scavenged battery stock, or picked up near wherever
  [Aether Breach at Briarwood](../encounters/aether-breach-at-briarwood.md)
  actually happened. Pairs naturally with the
  [Cindercrest Commendation Fang](cindercrest-commendation-fang.md) as
  loot from the same encounter family.
- **Hooks:** none required — a clean, satisfying damage spike a player can
  save for the fight that needs it.
- **Balance note:** a one-time damage add-on, not a persistent bonus — stays
  well inside the Uncommon "2nd-level spell effect, limited use" budget. Don't
  let it stack with itself; one crystal, one discharge.
