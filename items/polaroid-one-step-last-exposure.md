# Polaroid One Step, Last Exposure

*Wondrous Item, Uncommon*

As an action, you point this camera at a creature or object within 30 feet that you can see
or whose location you know, and press the shutter. The camera prints one photograph and then
falls silent forever. The photograph develops over the next minute. When it finishes, it shows
the subject exactly as it truly is: any illusion, disguise, or alteration of its form is absent
from the picture, and the photo also shows every invisible creature within 15 feet of the
subject, where they stood when the shutter clicked. The photo shows the subject as it was at
that moment and doesn't change after.

For 24 hours, you have advantage on any Wisdom (Survival) or Intelligence (Investigation) check
made to track, find, or identify the subject, so long as you can look at the photograph. The
photograph is an ordinary photograph after that.

## Flavor

A rainbow-striped Polaroid with a hairline crack across the viewfinder and a smear of Cinnabon
glaze on the strap. It was a birthday gift from a Waldenbooks employee to her nephew, and it has
carried one unexposed sheet of film for thirty years, which is not how film works. Every picture
it takes has somebody in the background: a drone, a camera operator, a small blurry shape
that turns out to be a lens. Nobody has ever managed to frame a shot without it.

## DM Notes

- **Why it's here:** plantable in any pregame set (the Sears garage is a natural), or on a
  Briarwood shelf as an ordinary find. Agent loves it for the on-camera photo that always
  catches *someone* in the frame.
- **Hooks:** the photographer in the background is always a different person. If the DM wants
  an ongoing thread, they could be real people, watching from somewhere.
- **Balance note:** it reveals, but it doesn't solve: it shows illusions, disguises and
  invisible creatures, but doesn't dispel them. Matches the ceiling of a 2nd-level spell
  (*See Invisibility*) used once.
