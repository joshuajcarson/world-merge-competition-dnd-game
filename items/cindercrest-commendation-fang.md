# Cindercrest Commendation Fang

*Wondrous Item, Uncommon*

This item has 3 charges and regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn.

**Early Warning.** While you wear this fang on a cord around your neck, you have
advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks made to hear or notice a creature that is
about to attack. If you succeed on such a check by 5 or more, you may immediately
use your reaction to warn one creature within 30 feet that can hear you; that
creature has advantage on the first saving throw or attack roll it makes against
the triggering threat.

**Run It to Ground.** As an action, you can expend 1 charge and speak the name of a
creature you have previously seen flee from combat while badly hurt. For the next
10 minutes, you sense the direction of that creature if it is within 1 mile,
though not its distance or condition. This property does nothing if the creature
has died.

## Flavor

The fang is longer than your forearm, root end still faintly warm, mounted crossways
through a disc of Administration-standard resin stamped **COHORT 23 · COMMENDATION ·
CONCOURSE INCIDENT, BRIARWOOD** in lettering too even to have been done by hand. It
does not smell like a fossil. It smells, faintly and permanently, like ozone and
fountain water — the two things that were in the air the night it came out of the
Cindercrest's jaw on the tile outside the "As Seen On TV" store, camera crews already
arriving before the last of the smoke off its wounds had cleared. Worn close to the
skin, it ticks — not audibly, just a pressure behind the sternum, once, whenever
something nearby is about to make the same mistake the Cindercrest's prey did.

## DM Notes

- **Why it's here:** the Administration's reward from
  [Aether Breach at Briarwood](../encounters/aether-breach-at-briarwood.md) — handed
  over on camera regardless of how the fight actually went, but the language on the
  accompanying broadcast segment (and the tone the production assistant or the
  feathered host uses in person) should reflect the table's Reputation & Broadcast
  read from that encounter: "rising stars" framing for a low-collateral, controlled
  fight, or a more clinical "asset worth monitoring" framing if the party plowed
  through bystanders to get it.
- **Hooks:** the Cindercrest itself may still be alive and loose somewhere in or
  around Bellcross if the encounter ended in **It Got Away** — this item's Run It
  to Ground property is a built-in rematch hook, not flavor text. If the encounter
  ended in **Contained** instead, the fang came from a live specimen, which the
  Administration would very much like back.
- **Balance note:** Run It to Ground only tracks creatures the party has personally
  seen flee while hurt — it isn't a general tracking spell, and it shouldn't be
  reflavored into one. Early Warning is a reactive, once-per-trigger read on danger,
  not a passive detect-threats effect; it needs a Perception check to fire.
