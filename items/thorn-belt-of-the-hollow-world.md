# Thorn Belt of the Hollow World

*Wondrous Item, Rare (Requires Attunement)*

This belt has 2 charges. It regains all expended charges daily at dawn. It also regains 1 expended charge whenever you reduce a creature to 0 hit points while wearing it, up to its maximum of 2.

**Gutting Blow.** When you hit a creature with a melee weapon attack, you can expend 1 charge to drive the belt's thorns into the wound. The target takes an extra 3d8 piercing damage and must succeed on a DC 15 Constitution saving throw or begin to bleed. A bleeding creature takes 1d8 piercing damage at the start of each of its turns. The bleeding ends when the creature regains any hit points, or when a creature uses an action to staunch the wound (a DC 10 Wisdom (Medicine) check, no check needed if they have a healer's kit). Constructs and undead are immune to the bleeding, but not to the extra damage.

## Flavor

A belt of braided green-black vine, studded with thorns that all curve inward toward the wearer and have never once drawn blood from them. It was made by a plant-folk in a world that never stopped fighting itself, a war between rooted nations that outlasted every treaty, every season, and every memory of what it started over. Their warlords each wore a belt like this and let it grow a thorn for every enemy they cut down. When the [Bar Bar Clan](../world/factions/bar-bar-clan.md) arrived, it did not take a side. It ended the war by ending all of them, and the belt was the one thing there the clan kept, because it was the only thing still growing. It still keeps count: after a fight, it drops one dead leaf for each creature its wearer killed.

## DM Notes

- **Why it's here:** it is the clan's trophy, carried by one of the [Bar-Bar Brutes](../encounters/the-underspill.md) in The Underspill. The clan took it from a world it invaded and wiped out, so it's a prize any Bar-Bar would be proud of.
- **Hooks:** the plant-folk and their world are gone, and nobody in the party has heard of either. The name "Hollow World" is only what the belt is called now, so decide who named it, or leave it unexplained. Agent would love to bring up how the clan "ended an entire war." What exactly the Bar-Bar did there is the kind of question that makes the clan sound more dangerous.
- **The leaves:** the kill-leaves are only flavor. If the party asks, they match the kill count and nothing else.
- **Balance note:** at 3d8 plus a bleed of 1d8 per turn, a charge is roughly the output of a 3rd-level spell, which is what a Rare item can carry. It adds no accuracy and no AC. Because kills refund charges, a fighter who keeps killing can use it nearly every round against weak enemies, and that is the intended feel. Bleeding does not stack. If it's too strong, make the bleed need a Medicine check from the target's ally to end, or drop it to 1d6. A bump to Very Rare would allow 4d8 and a stacking bleed.
