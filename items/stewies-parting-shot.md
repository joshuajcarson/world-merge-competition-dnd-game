# Stewie's Parting Shot

*Weapon (thrown flask), Uncommon*

This weighted clay flask can be thrown as a weapon. Make a ranged
attack roll against a point you can see within 30 feet (treat this as a
ranged weapon attack; you add your proficiency bonus only if you're
proficient with improvised weapons). On a hit, or a miss that lands
within 5 feet of the target point, the flask shatters: each creature
within 10 feet of that point must make a DC 13 Dexterity saving throw,
taking 3d6 fire damage on a failed save or half as much on a successful
one. Unattended flammable objects in the area catch fire.

## Flavor

A stoppered clay flask, [the Stewies](../world/factions/the-stewies.md)'
own tattoo — the one the party calls "Stevie Griffin" — scratched crudely
into the wax seal, like whoever packed it wanted credit even in a supply
crate no officer would ever open. It's heavier than it looks, and it
sloshes wrong, like the liquid inside is still deciding whether to be
angry about something.

## DM Notes

- **Why it's here:** scavenged from the Stewies' abandoned mercenary-
  district camp after [Session 2](../sessions/02-the-mall-king.md), or
  looted off a scattered company member later.
- **Hooks:** the flask's make is a physical clue, if the party ever wants
  to trace where the Stewies got their gear back to whichever
  [Coster Council](../world/factions/coster-council.md) member actually
  funded them.
- **Balance note:** single-use thrown weapon, 3d6 fire in a 10-foot
  radius — sits right at Uncommon's ceiling. Don't let the party stockpile
  more than the one found copy without a real in-fiction source.
