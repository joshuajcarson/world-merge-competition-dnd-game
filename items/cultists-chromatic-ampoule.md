# Cultist's Chromatic Ampoule

*Potion, Uncommon*

As an action, you can throw this ampoule up to 30 feet, shattering it on
impact. Each creature in a 10-foot radius of the point you chose must make a
DC 13 Dexterity saving throw, taking 4d6 Acid damage on a failed save, or
half as much on a successful one.

## Flavor

Cloudy green glass, stoppered with cult wax stamped in the shape of a head
that isn't the one on the banners anymore — standard-issue for whichever
chromatic auxiliary got assigned this color this campaign season. Shake it
and something inside moves like it's still deciding whether it's alive.

## DM Notes

- **Why it's here:** [Cult of Tiamat](../world/factions/cult-of-tiamat.md)
  logistics — the coast's new administrators run supply chains, and this is
  what those supply chains issue. Plausible off any cultist patrol or
  requisition cache, not a unique find.
- **Hooks:** the wax seal's head shape is a small, optional clue to which
  chromatic auxiliary issued it, if a DM wants to seed which part of the
  conquered coast a cache came from.
- **Balance note:** mirrors the *Potion of Fire Breath* anchor exactly
  (DC 13, 4d6, 10-foot burst, once), reskinned to acid — deliberately green
  rather than red, to keep it visually and thematically distinct from the
  [Aether Crystal Shard](aether-crystal-shard.md) in the same batch.
