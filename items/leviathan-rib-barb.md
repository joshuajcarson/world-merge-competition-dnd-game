# Leviathan Rib-Barb

*Weapon (Thrown), Uncommon*

You can hurl this barb as a ranged weapon attack (range 30/90 feet). You are proficient with it. On
a hit, the target takes 4d6 piercing damage, and if it is Large or smaller, the barb lodges in it
and the target is restrained. A restrained creature can use its action to make a DC 13 Strength check,
pulling the barb out and freeing itself on a success. If the target is Huge or larger, its speed is
reduced by 10 feet for 1 minute instead.

The barb is spent after one throw. A barb that hits crumbles to chalk when pulled out, and one that
misses is ruined.

## Flavor

It is a curved, hand-length hook of pale bone, still faintly warm, grown into a ridge of
mold-green growth at the base. It came out of a leviathan's rib cage on the Aether World's side
of the merge, and the beast that grew it never had to hunt: whatever was attracted to the
glow only had to come close. When it is hefted, a sound can be heard inside the bone like
a very small bell being rung at the bottom of the sea.

## DM Notes

- **Why it's here:** an Aether-side drop: left on a battlesite, found in a scavenger's den, or
  pulled from a corpse the [Sextonbacks](../world/races/sextonbacks.md) overlooked. Sextonbacks
  ignore it, which is a nice small tell.
- **Hooks:** the bell sound in the bone. If the table wants more, the leviathan is still
  out there.
- **Balance note:** 4d6 is a single, strong hit, and the restrain gives a party a real way to hold
  something still for a round or two. Huge targets get only a speed penalty on purpose.
