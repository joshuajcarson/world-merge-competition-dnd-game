# Council Evacuation Flare

*Wondrous Item, Rare*

As a bonus action, ignite this flare and choose a point on the ground
you can see within 60 feet. You and up to three willing creatures within
10 feet of you instantly teleport to that point. The flare crumbles to
ash once used.

## Flavor

A brass tube stenciled COSTER EVACUATION — BARGE MUSTER POINT 4, meant to
summon a boat that was supposed to be waiting at a dock that, as far as
anyone in Scornubel now knows, no longer exists on this side of the
merge. Struck against a hard surface, it still ignites — a hard white
spark, no smoke, no sound — and for one heartbeat it seems to be looking
for the barge it was built to call, before it settles for wherever you
actually are.

## DM Notes

- **Why it's here:** [Coster Council](../world/factions/coster-council.md)
  stockpile, originally meant for the provisioned evacuation barges
  mentioned in that faction's own DM notes — a plausible find in
  [Scornubel](../world/places/scornubel.md)'s wagon-yards or Council Hall.
- **Hooks:** implies the barges, and Muster Point 4 specifically, existed
  somewhere real — a loose thread if the DM wants Scornubel's pre-merge
  evacuation plan to matter later.
- **Balance note:** short range (60-foot target, 10-foot gather radius),
  single use, no damage — a pure mobility/escape tool. Keep it that way;
  don't let a later draft add damage on top of the teleport.
