# Meridian Peak Field Stimulant

*Potion, Rare*

As a bonus action, you drink this vial or administer it to a creature
within 5 feet of you. The target regains 8d4 + 8 hit points, and if it
was at 0 hit points, it immediately regains consciousness at its new
total without spending a Hit Die. This vial's contents lose their potency
1 hour after the seal is broken.

## Flavor

A glass ampoule, brand-new, sealed in plastic stamped with a compound
crest neither Faerun nor Earth has a name for on its own — MERIDIAN PEAK
WELLNESS COLLECTIVE, and beneath it, smaller: VIABILITY-CONFIRMED STOCK
ONLY. It's real medicine, manufactured, tested, the kind Bellcross hasn't
had access to since the grand opening — which is exactly why someone in
the holding pen had it taken away from them before the party ever found
it. It goes warm in the hand right before you'd need it, like it already
knows.

## DM Notes

- **Why it's here:** recovered from
  [Meridian Peak](../world/factions/meridian-peak.md)'s manifest or
  holding-pen stock — see
  [the Color Game](../encounters/the-color-game.md)'s Node D and
  [The Long Climb](../encounters/the-long-climb.md). A specific, earned
  reward for engaging that plot thread rather than generic loot; don't
  hand this out anywhere else without a reason.
- **Hooks:** there's more where this came from, guarded, wherever
  Meridian Peak's actual supply chain runs — a strong reason to go looking
  for them again later.
- **Balance note:** single-use, roughly *Potion of Superior Healing*
  numbers — a real clutch save, not a renewable resource. Ration it as
  loot, not shop stock.
