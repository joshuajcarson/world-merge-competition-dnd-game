# Cohort 18 Slag Ring

*Ring, Rare (Requires Attunement)*

You have Resistance to Fire damage while wearing this ring.

The ring has 3 charges and regains 1d6 + 1 expended charges daily at dawn — but it regains no charges on any day you have not taken Fire damage.

**Vent.** As a Magic action, you can expend 1 charge to release the heat the ring has banked. Each creature in a 10-foot Emanation originating from you must make a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw, taking 4d6 Fire damage on a failed save or half as much on a successful one. You take no damage from this effect.

## Flavor

The band is not jewelry so much as a piece of something larger that cooled into this shape: grey-white slag with a seam of green running through it where two metals that should never have touched decided to. It came up out of the recovery pull from Cohort 18 — the low-magic world the Administration lost, the one that ran hot and melted — and there is a serial number stamped on the inside curve that belongs to a contestant whose file the Administration will not open. It stays faintly warm at all times, warmer near open flame, and it has never once been warm at the same moment its wearer felt safe.

## DM Notes

- **Why it's here:** salvage-market goods, Administration surplus, or the personal effects of someone who worked recovery on a previous cohort. Fits any hoard that should feel *bureaucratic* rather than mythic.
- **Hooks:** the serial number is traceable. Someone in the Administration would prefer it weren't — a cover-up is exactly the kind of soulless institutional reflex the Administration runs on. [Agent](../world/npcs/agent.md) might not agree; a buried scandal is bad television, and Agent likes drama a great deal more than it likes doing what the Administration wants. A player who pushes on it is pulling on what actually happened to Cohort 18 — and, potentially, on that exact tension.
- **Balance note:** the no-Fire-damage-means-no-recharge clause is the price of a Rare-tier resistance plus a damage burst. Don't quietly drop it; it's what keeps this inside budget.
