# Fire Extinguisher

*Wondrous Item, Uncommon*

As an action, discharge this extinguisher in a 15-foot cone. Each
creature in the area must make a DC 13 Constitution saving throw, taking
3d6 cold damage on a failed save, or half as much on a successful one.
The cone extinguishes all nonmagical fire in the area and heavily
obscures it with white vapor until the end of your next turn. The
extinguisher is spent after one use.

## Flavor

Red enamel, a pressure gauge stuck permanently in the green, and an
inspection tag last punched two months before the grand opening —
Briarwood's own, pulled off a wall bracket by someone who never got the
chance to need it for an ordinary fire. It hisses white even when nothing's
burning, like it's decided extinguishing is just something it does now,
fire optional.

## DM Notes

- **Why it's here:** Briarwood Mall's own back-of-house, or any other
  pre-merge Earth structure with standard safety equipment — an easy,
  plausible find scattered through service corridors, storerooms, and
  mechanical rooms.
- **Hooks:** none forced — a clean, low-drama way to hand the party a cold
  damage answer for a real emergency without needing an ice-magic NPC to
  explain it.
- **Balance note:** single-shot, so expect the party to save it rather
  than spend it casually — that's the intended use pattern.
