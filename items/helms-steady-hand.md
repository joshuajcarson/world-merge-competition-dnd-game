# Helm's Steady Hand

*Wondrous Item, Common*

This small iron token, stamped with an eye inside a gauntlet, can be
used once. As a reaction when you are hit by an attack, you can crush
the token to reduce the damage you take from that attack by 1d10.

## Flavor

Stamped from a shilling too worn to spend, sold off a Shrine Row cart for
whatever a buyer could spare — Helm doesn't need the coin, the vendor
always says, Helm needs the devotion. It goes ice-cold in your palm
exactly once, right before the hit that would have hurt worse.

## DM Notes

- **Why it's here:** [Scornubel](../world/places/scornubel.md)'s Shrine
  Row — a cheap, plausible pickup for a party passing through, or a gift
  from a grateful refugee with nothing else to give.
- **Hooks:** none forced — a fine, disposable "one more reason to survive
  this fight" item.
- **Balance note:** single reactive use, flat 1d10 reduction, no
  persistent bonus — genuinely Common, no build-around potential.
