# Protein Shake

*Potion, Common*

As a Bonus Action, you drink this shake or administer it to another creature
within 5 feet of yourself. That creature regains 2d4 + 2 hit points. The can
is then empty, and stays empty.

## Flavor

Still sealed in its GNC pull-tab can, except the shake inside has gone the
color of a healing bruise — yellow-green, faintly glowing — and it separates
back into a smooth suspension no matter how hard you shake it first. One of
maybe forty identical cans still racked by the register the night everything
changed.

## DM Notes

- **Why it's here:** GNC's entire shelf stock, functionally — this isn't a
  unique find, it's what the store's inventory *is* now. Scatter several
  through Briarwood as ordinary environmental loot rather than treating any
  single can as special.
- **Hooks:** none needed. It's a Potion of Healing that happens to have a
  barcode.
- **Balance note:** identical to the Potion of Healing anchor (2d4+2) on
  purpose — no invented math, no reason to make Earth's version stronger or
  weaker than the fantasy-world original.
