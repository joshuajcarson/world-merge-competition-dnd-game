# Mood Ring

*Ring, Common*

While wearing this ring, you can take a Magic action to ask one question of a
creature you can see within 30 feet. That creature must succeed on a DC 13
Wisdom saving throw or answer honestly and truthfully in its very next
statement, as far as it knows the truth — it is not compelled to answer at
length, or to answer at all, only to not lie if it does. The stone then
clouds over gray, and the ring loses its magic.

## Flavor

The stone cycles through its whole color chart in about four seconds now,
faster than anyone's mood actually changes, and settles on a color the box's
little mood chart never listed the one time it's asked something that
matters. A dollar ninety-nine off the spinner rack by the Spencer Gifts
register, before the till stopped mattering.

## DM Notes

- **Why it's here:** Spencer Gifts' spinner rack, same shelf-stock logic as
  the [Protein Shake](protein-shake.md) — treat any given ring as one of
  several identical ones on the rack, not a unique artifact.
- **Hooks:** a single truthful answer, chosen by the DM to actually matter
  when it lands, is a strong small payoff for a party that's been fed nothing
  but rumor and panic — good to save for a moment where one honest sentence
  changes the table's plan.
- **Balance note:** capped at one question, ever, with a save attached — this
  is a sliver of *Zone of Truth*, not the spell itself, and shouldn't be
  reflavored into a repeatable lie detector.
