# Dessarin Ford Seeker Arrow

*Weapon (Arrow), Uncommon*

You have a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic arrow. When you fire it from
a bow, choose a creature you have seen within the last minute that is within the weapon's range,
even if you can't see it now. The arrow ignores half cover and three-quarters cover for the
attack, curving around obstacles on the way to the target. If it hits, the target is outlined in
silver light for 1 minute: it can't benefit from being invisible, and it sheds dim light in a
10-foot radius.

Once fired, the arrow turns to a puff of silver dust, hit or miss.

## Flavor

The fletching is goose and the shaft is polished to the color of the river it was cut beside;
the head is a leaf of plain steel, none too sharp. It came off a hunting-party quiver in the
Dessarin Valley before the dragon cult took the river road, and its maker's mark is a small
carved ford. If you hold it up to the light, the shaft always points toward whoever you were
last thinking about, which gets awkward.

## DM Notes

- **Why it's here:** Faerun-side loot, easy to plant in Scornubel's wagon-yards, on a Coster
  Council archer, or in the Cult of the Swan's stolen stock. It could be Agent's pick for a
  pregame reward in a fight with someone who hides.
- **Hooks:** the maker's ford mark. Who made it, and what hunt was it cut for?
- **Balance note:** a +1 arrow that wastes itself on any shot. The cover bypass and the
  can't-hide rider are the value, and neither damages anyone.
