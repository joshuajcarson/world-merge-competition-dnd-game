# Jack's Grappling Hook

*Wondrous Item, Common*

A reusable grapple and cable, hand-built rather than found. As an action,
you can fire it at a fixed point within 60 feet you can see and haul
yourself (and, with a DC 10 Strength check, one other creature holding
onto you) across a gap or up to that point. It's rope and hardware, not
magic — treat any DC as an ordinary Athletics check for the situation, not
a fixed number this file sets.

## Flavor

Salvaged pulleys, a coil of climbing rope nobody's claimed, and a hook
Jack reshaped from something that used to be a tow hitch. It has already
gone over more open water than it was built for and hasn't failed yet,
which Jack takes as a professional insult every time.

## DM Notes

- **Why it's here:** built on the spot during
  [Session 6](../sessions/06-the-fixed-game.md) to cross the Aether Tide's
  open water to the Wells Fargo Regional Office. A field-expedient build,
  not a polished long-term project like
  [Jack's Spectrometer](jacks-spectrometer.md) or [The R2 Rig](r2-rig.md).
- **Balance note:** no combat application, no fixed DC — a traversal tool
  the DM adjudicates contextually, not a mechanical subsystem.
