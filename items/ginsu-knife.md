# Ginsu Knife

*Weapon (any simple bladed weapon), Common*

As an action, make a single impossibly precise cut: choose one creature or
object you can see within 5 feet. If a creature, it must succeed on a DC 13
Dexterity saving throw or take 4d6 slashing damage (half as much on a
success). If an object, the knife cuts cleanly through up to 3 feet of
nonmagical material with no roll required. Either way, the blade's edge dulls
to ordinary steel immediately afterward.

## Flavor

The infomercial promised it would stay sharp through a shoe, a tomato, and a
penny, in that order, and for one cut it actually can — the edge doesn't
catch light, doesn't dull, and goes exactly where it's pointed, through
whatever's actually in the way. Still in its as-seen-on-TV blister pack, one
of the display units off the grand opening's card table.

## DM Notes

- **Why it's here:** the "As Seen On TV" store's card table display, alongside
  the Chop-O-Matics and Miracle Blades that (for now) are just knives — this
  is the one that happened to be magical, not a signal the whole product line
  is.
- **Hooks:** none needed. A satisfying, cinematic finisher for a low-level
  fight, not a build-around.
- **Balance note:** mirrors the real *Potion of Fire Breath* anchor (DC 13,
  4d6, once) rather than inventing new math — same power level, reskinned
  slashing instead of fire, weapon instead of potion.
