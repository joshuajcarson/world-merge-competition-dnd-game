# Bug Spray

*Wondrous Item, Common*

As an action, spray this can in a 15-foot cone. Each vermin, insect swarm,
or parasitic creature (Small or smaller) in the area must succeed on a
DC 13 Constitution saving throw or take 2d6 poison damage and have
disadvantage on attack rolls until the end of its next turn. Creatures
that aren't vermin, swarms, or parasites are unaffected beyond a strong
chemical smell. The can is empty after one use.

## Flavor

The label's half peeled, the brand unreadable under a sticker reading
DOES NOT WORK ON WHAT'S OUT THERE NOW — CONFIRMED, in a hand nobody's
identified. It smells exactly like it always did, right up until the
worms start dying.

## DM Notes

- **Why it's here:** any plausible post-merge hardware or grocery aisle —
  Briarwood's own stock, or handed off by a resident who's already seen it
  work. A fair drop anywhere vermin or parasitic corruption has been an
  issue (the Fearus Dungeon's gem-corrupted attackers, the Session 3
  corrupted puma's worms).
- **Hooks:** the label's "confirmed" implies someone field-tested this and
  lived to write it down — a small, optional NPC or thread if the DM wants
  one; not required to make the item work.
- **Balance note:** narrow and situational by design — only matters against
  vermin/swarm/parasite-type threats, which is what keeps real damage
  legal at Common.
