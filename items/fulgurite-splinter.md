# Fulgurite Splinter

*Wondrous Item, Uncommon*

This item can be used once. As a Bonus Action, you can crush the splinter,
consuming it, to release the charge trapped inside — choose one effect below.
Each creature within 10 feet of you (other than you) must succeed on the
listed DC 13 saving throw or suffer the effect:

- **Force surge.** Strength save or be pushed 10 feet and knocked prone.
- **Psychic scream.** Wisdom save or be frightened of you until the end of
  your next turn.
- **Arc-light.** Constitution save or be blinded until the end of your next
  turn.

Once used, the splinter shatters into fine, cooling grit and can't be used
again.

## Flavor

A finger-length shard of what should be black glass, except it's warm to hold
and there's a hairline vein of white-blue light still moving inside it,
unhurried, like it hasn't found the way out yet. It's what's left when a
[Fulgurite](../world/races/fulgurites.md)'s [Overcharge](../world/races/fulgurites.md)
finally uses the person up — the Underdark's scavengers have learned to cut
these free of the calcified remains before the body's gone, because they sell.
It ticks, faintly, at the pace of a fast and frightened heartbeat, right up
until the moment it's crushed.

## DM Notes

- **Why it's here:** Underdark black-market salvage — the kind of thing the
  [Ashvein Company](../world/factions/ashvein-company.md) trades in alongside
  captives, and a plausible drop from any Fulgurite-adjacent encounter where
  someone doesn't walk away (looted off [the Chorus](../world/npcs/the-chorus.md)
  is especially grim, given how many splinters that might actually yield). A
  sympathetic Fulgurite — [Priya Anand](../world/npcs/priya-anand.md), say —
  handing one over is a different, heavier kind of gift than finding one on a
  duergar corpse; consider which fits the scene before deciding how the party
  gets it.
- **Hooks:** using it is recycling a piece of a person who didn't consent to
  any of this, and some NPCs (Priya, certainly) will have a visible reaction
  to seeing one used or traded. Good friction for a party that's been treating
  Fulgurites as a monster-of-the-week rather than as people the merge did this
  to.
- **Balance note:** single use, no recharge, no attunement, no persistent
  bonus — this is an Uncommon consumable on the *Potion of Healing* model, not
  a build-defining pickup. DC 13 matches the Uncommon tier's typical item save
  DC and mirrors the Fulgurite racial trait exactly, so it never needs its own
  math. Don't let a party stockpile these into a reliable AoE battery; keep
  supply scarce and grim, in keeping with what they cost to make.
