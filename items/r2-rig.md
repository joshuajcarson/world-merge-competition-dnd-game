# The R2 Rig

*Armor (splint), Uncommon*

Functions as ordinary splint armor (AC 17, Strength 15 required,
disadvantage on Stealth checks), plus:

**Built-In Toolkit.** While wearing this armor, you have a +2 bonus to
Intelligence (Investigation) checks. The armor's plates are studded with
salvaged lenses, dial gauges, and a rat's nest of wiring that Jack actually
uses, not just wears.

## Flavor

Splint mail, hand-fitted to a Goliath frame and then, over the course of a
long-term project, steadily bolted over with everything Jack couldn't bear
to leave in a junk pile — a domed inspection lamp over one shoulder, a
cracked rearview mirror repurposed as a rangefinder, blinking indicator
lights wired to nothing anyone's identified yet. The table named it before
Jack finished building it: he looks, unmistakably, like a very large, very
armored astromech droid.

## DM Notes

- **Why it's here:** the result of one of Jack's long-term projects — this
  campaign is running Blades in the Dark-style downtime projects between
  sessions, and this was his, alongside
  [Jack's Spectrometer](jacks-spectrometer.md) from the same stretch of
  downtime.
- **Balance note:** a flat, always-on skill bonus rather than a combat
  effect — appropriate for a Tier 1-2 Uncommon utility item. No charges, no
  attunement; it's armor Jack built, not a magic item he found.
