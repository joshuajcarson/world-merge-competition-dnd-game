# Tymora's Last Copper

*Wondrous Item, Uncommon*

When you fail an ability check, attack roll, or saving throw, you can spend
this coin (no action required) to reroll it, using the new result. The coin
then tarnishes black and loses its magic.

## Flavor

A bent traveler's-shrine copper off Scornubel's Shrine Row, Tymora's
coin-flip stamped into it so worn you have to already know it's there to see
it. Every roadside shrine on that street sells a dozen of these a day, and
nobody who buys one really expects it to work. This one, once, still does.

## DM Notes

- **Why it's here:** [Scornubel](../world/places/scornubel.md)'s Shrine
  Row — cheap devotional stock, sold alongside a dozen other roadside gods'
  tokens by people who need the two coppers more than the luck. A natural
  first find for the party's first real Faerun-side exploration.
- **Hooks:** whether it's genuinely Tymora's favor or just a fifty-fifty coin
  that happened to land right is a nice ambiguity to leave open at the
  table — this is a "gods who have already lost" setting, and one working
  blessing says something about faith outliving conquest either way.
- **Balance note:** a single reroll, no advantage stacking — the new result
  is used even if it's worse, same as *Lucky* and similar reroll effects.
  One coin, one use, then it's just a bent copper again.
